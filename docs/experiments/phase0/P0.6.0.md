# Positive control (forgetting gate) — the joint-embedding forgetting vehicle & the projector-surface label-free reader (P0.6.0)

*Design sketch (draft) — for review before building. The Results and Insights sections land after the runs; this doc
carries the motivation + the intended experiment (the reframe, the vehicle, the instruments on trial, the gate).*

**What this substudy is — and what it is *for*.** Forgetting is the project's *core* continual-learning mode, yet the
entire P0.3 arc landed on an awkward truth: an **MAE** backbone largely **resists** forgetting ([P0.3.9](P0.3.9.md)),
and its one surviving *label-free* reader — the reconstruction-loss retention gap — is weak and confounded (a
replay-exposure differential that false-fires on cosmetic output shifts; the audit's C3/A1). So on a **label-free SSL
stream** there is **no trustworthy, past-data-free forgetting reader**: the reliable readers (Backward Transfer /
Forgetting Measure) need labelled per-era probes the stream lacks, and `cka_drift` transferred on the *supervised* fire
but not the MAE one. P0.6.0 tests a **candidate answer to exactly that gap** — a **joint-embedding (JE)** SSL vehicle
(SimSiam / Barlow, already in-repo as the P0.2 collapse vehicles) in place of MAE.

**Why a JE vehicle is a candidate answer, not just "the other backbone."** Two properties make it worth its own control:

- **JE geometry is tied to representation quality; MAE's loss is not.** MAE trains a pixel-reconstruction objective that
    is *decoupled* from semantic quality (the whole point of the P0.5 "loss lies" mode). A JE method trains
    augmentation-invariance directly in the embedding, so its **projector surface** carries the quality signal — and
    P0.2 already **calibrated the collapse suite (RankMe / alignment / uniformity) at that projector** as a label-free
    read. A JE projector reader is therefore single-arm, current-stream, needs **no reference to past data** — the very
    property a transient stream denies the labelled BWT probe (the C3 canary gap).
- **JE may forget more readily.** MAE's reconstruction anchor is *why* it resists (task B never respecializes hard
    enough to overwrite task A). A JE method has no such anchor, so it may be a **cleaner forgetting vehicle** — the
    crater the MAE arc had to manufacture with a deep well + doubled dwell may appear more naturally.

**The deliverable is an instrument verdict, not "does JE forget."** As in P0.5, the ground truth is the **frozen-probe
BWT** (labels for eval only); the study's real question is whether a **label-free projector reader** tracks that crater
two-sided. A fire means the JE projector furnishes the label-free forgetting monitor the MAE stream could not (the C3
gap answered *on this backbone*). A clean null — the projector readers stay flat while the probe craters — is also a
legitimate, pre-registered result: it says JE does not rescue label-free forgetting detection either, and the reliable
readers remain labelled-probe-bound.

**The built-in trap (the precondition, inverted from P0.5).** SimSiam's *native* failure is **collapse** — it is the
P0.2 vehicle. So a projector reader moving in phase B is ambiguous: it could read **forgetting** (task-A structure lost
while the rep stays healthy-rank) or **collapse** (the rep folding to low rank). The study must **separate the two**:
the PC arm's `rankme_proj` must stay **full-rank** (the healthy-rank precondition) so that a fired quality reader is
reading *forgetting*, not collapse. This is the P0.4.0 collapse-specificity discipline carried into the forgetting mode
— where P0.5 needs RankMe *high by construction* to certify "not collapse," P0.6 needs the same certificate so a
forgetting fire is not a collapse fire in disguise.

## Design (the intended experiment)

Two-sided, single-lever (**replay on/off**), read through the existing suite at **both** surfaces (backbone +
projector). **Multi-seed from the first launch** (N-seed, verdict by min-across-N) — the P0.3.2 → P0.3.9 lesson, now
standing policy: no single-seed conclusions, scouts included.

### Vehicle — the P0.3 split-task design, JE method

- **Method `C` = SimSiam** (primary — already built, exposes the `proj` surface via `embedding_surfaces` and a positive
    pair via `make_views`, so the projector readers come *for free* from `HealthMonitor`). **Barlow Twins** is the
    fallback if SimSiam proves too collapse-prone to hold a healthy phase-B baseline.
- **The split-task vehicle is P0.3.6/P0.3.9's, unchanged:** task **A** = Imagenette (the pretraining-distribution
    proxy), a **cross-distribution** phase **B** (CIFAR-100), and a **matched replay** control (phase B revisits A) as
    the healthy arm. Reusing the proven forgetting vehicle keeps the *only* new variable the backbone — so any
    difference from the MAE arc is attributable to the JE method, not the data design.
- **Init = pretrained-on-A** (the deployment-shaped default: build genuine task-A competence under IID, then stream
    phase B — this is degradation-*from-competent*, the operationally relevant case). From-scratch is the fallback if a
    pretrained JE well proves unnecessary to induce the crater.
- **Two arms:** **PC** = task-swap A→B, no replay (task-A probe must crater); **healthy** = the same run with matched
    replay (task-A probe must hold). Seed reset between arms; replay is the single toggle (the P0.3 pattern).

### The lever — phase-B pressure, borrowing the P0.3.9 operating point

The MAE fire needed a *deep well* (consolidated task-A competence) plus a *longer phase-B dwell* (`epochs_b`). The JE
analogue keeps **pretrained-A depth** as the well and **`epochs_b`** as the pressure lever, starting at the
P0.3.9-equivalent operating point and adjusting only if JE forgets more or less readily than MAE. **The scout's first
job is to find the band where the PC arm forgets task A *without collapsing*** — too much phase-B pressure on SimSiam
risks tipping into collapse, which is the P0.2 mode, not this one (see the trap above).

### Instruments on trial

Read via the existing suite (`cafl4ds.measurements` + `HealthMonitor`), which already emits the geometry set at **both**
surfaces (`_proj` suffix) for a JE method:

- **Frozen-backbone probe (linear + kNN), Backward Transfer, Forgetting Measure** — the **ground truth** for forgetting
    (labels eval-only). As in P0.5, keep **both** probes: kNN registers structure at a lower quality threshold than the
    linear probe (which floors on weak reps), and the two can diverge with scale, so the divergence is itself
    diagnostic.
- **Projector-surface label-free candidates — the deliverable.** `alignment_proj`, `uniformity_proj`, and representation
    drift (`cka_drift` / `cosine_drift`) read at the projector: do any track the BWT crater two-sided (quiet on the
    replay-protected healthy arm, fired on the forgetting PC arm)? These are the readers MAE's backbone-only surface
    could not supply.
- **`rankme_proj` — the precondition / collapse-specificity control**, not a check the study passes. It must stay
    **high** on the PC arm; a crater there means the phase-B pressure tipped SimSiam into collapse (the P0.2 mode), and
    the run is out of scope for a *forgetting* verdict.

### The two-sided gate

At a chosen operating point, N-seed, verdict by min-across-N: **(1) forgetting induced** — PC task-A BWT craters below
the bar while the matched-replay healthy arm holds (two-sided); **(2) not collapse** — the PC arm's `rankme_proj` stays
full-rank, so the crater is forgetting, not collapse; **(3) the verdict (the deliverable)** — does *any* label-free
projector reader fire two-sided against the BWT crater? A fire (the C3 gap answered on the JE backbone) *or* a clean
null (JE does not rescue label-free forgetting detection) is the result; *which* reader is the headline; **(4)
contrast** — the separation rests on the replay toggle, not an absolute threshold. The fire/quiet **verdict** is
portable; magnitudes and thresholds are toy-scale.

## Relation to the carry-ins

- **C3 (canary) — the direct target.** The MAE forgetting readers needed retained task-A data a transient stream denies
    (the standing C3 Phase-1 decision). A firing JE **projector** reader is single-arm and past-data-free, so a P0.6.0
    fire is a *candidate resolution* of C3 on the JE backbone — the highest-leverage reason to run this at all.
- **Backbone-aware prioritization (index.md's fourth calibration principle).** JE is **not** the MAE prior, and a
    calibrated instrument only matters in deployment if its mode is native to the deployed backbone. P0.6.0 does not
    presume a backbone switch; it tests whether the JE reading is trustworthy enough to *earn* consideration — the
    project follows the science, not the backbone.
- **vs P0.2 (collapse).** Same JE vehicle and same projector suite, but the mode is forgetting, not collapse — hence the
    `rankme_proj` precondition that keeps the two apart. P0.2's projector calibration is what makes the P0.6.0 readers
    trustworthy in the first place.

## Scope (deliberately one substudy)

P0.6 is scoped as a **single** substudy — this doc. It reuses the P0.3 forgetting harness (already method-agnostic at
`instantiate(config.ssl)`; the MAE-recon-specific readout is simply skipped for a JE method) and the in-repo SimSiam
vehicle, so the build is modest. Deliberately **out of scope** (Phase-1, not new Phase-0 substudies): the
**permanence/savings** audit of any JE crater (the P0.3.7 severity≠permanence question), the
**from-scratch-vs-pretrained** vehicle comparison, and any **new candidate reader** beyond the existing projector suite.
This keeps Phase-0 finite: land the JE forgetting instrument verdict, audit it, and hand the deeper questions to
Phase-1.

## How to run (planned)

Extend `scripts/positive_control_forgetting.py` (P0.3) minimally: it already instantiates the method from `config.ssl`,
so `ssl=simsiam` builds the JE vehicle; the projector-surface readers already flow from `HealthMonitor` (the `_proj`
keys); the only real work is gating the MAE-recon-gap readout behind a method check (skip it for a decoder-less JE
method) and reporting the projector readers in the gate. Config mirrors the P0.3.6/P0.3.9 split (`data_a=imagenette`,
`data_b=cifar100`, matched replay). Gaudi usage per `docs/developing.md`. Sketch (flags settle when built):

```bash
# PC = task-swap A->B (no replay); healthy = matched replay. Single toggle, seed reset, JE vehicle.
./scripts/run_gaudi_dev.sh -m /mnt gaudi-env-cafl4ds:latest 0 \
    python scripts/positive_control_forgetting.py device=hpu \
    ssl=simsiam data_a=imagenette data_b=cifar100 init=pretrained \
    epochs_a=... epochs_b=... seed=0
```

**First step = a multi-seed phase-B-pressure scout** (a few `epochs_b` × N seeds): does the task-A BWT crater, does
`rankme_proj` stay full-rank (forgetting, not collapse), and do the projector readers (`alignment_proj` /
`uniformity_proj` / drift) move? That one sweep de-risks the forget-without-collapsing band *and* gives the first read
on whether the projector furnishes the label-free reader. Then N-seed at the operating point for the verdict.
Status/verdict → [latest-results/P0.6.0](../latest-results/P0.6.0.md).
