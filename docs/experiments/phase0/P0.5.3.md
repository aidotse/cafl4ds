# Positive control (representation-health gate) — the quality-vs-lever test for `alignment_strong`: a decoder dose-down (P0.5.3)

**What this substudy is — and what it is *for*.** [P0.5.2](P0.5.2.md) delivered a candidate MAE-native quality reader —
`alignment` under a stronger augment — that fires two-sided (5/5) on a genuine full-rank degradation where the whole
P0.2 geometry suite is blind or mis-signals. The one thing it could not settle is the thing that decides whether the
reader is *deployable*: does it read representation quality (the crater — so it would fire on *any* real degradation),
or does it read the over-powered-decoder lever's geometry (a vehicle fingerprint a Phase-1 stream will not carry)? This
is the mode's central open item — P0.5 was opened to find an MAE-native quality reader, so whether its one candidate is
even a quality reader *is* the constructive deliverable. It is the audit's C1
([`../audits/P0.5.md`](../audits/P0.5.md)), promoted from a Phase-1 hand-off to a bounded Phase-0 run.

**Why a *light* confirmation, not a full study — P0.5.2 already leans hard.** The existing crater-hunt grid points three
ways to "reads the decoder, not quality," and P0.5.3 exists only to remove the one confound left standing:

1. At the *fixed* over-powered decoder, the fire is flat (0.063–0.083) while the kNN crater swings 2.7× across seeds
    (0.028–0.076) — at fixed lever, the fire is decoupled from how much quality was lost.
1. Two decoder seeds crater 0.028 / 0.032 — *shallower* than the mask lever's 0.042 — yet fire (0.063 / 0.064), while
    the *deeper* mask crater sits quiet at 0.030. A shallower crater firing and a deeper one not, split by lever,
    already decouples the fire from crater depth.
1. Once rank erodes (e64) the fire drops to 0.001 despite a 0.066 crater — it tracks geometry, not the crater.

The single wrinkle is that the shallow firing craters in (2) — 0.028, 0.032 — sit within ~1× of the probe-noise floor
(null ≈ 0.024), so they *might* be noisy readouts of a deeper latent degradation rather than genuinely mild ones. A
deliberate dose-down replaces that noisy accident with a controlled point, converting a strong lean into a clean
verdict.

## The test — a decoder dose-*down* (trace the fire against the crater)

One clean axis: weaken the shortcut arm's decoder in steps from the firing 512×8 toward the healthy 64×2, holding
everything else at the P0.5.2 operating point (embed 96, from-scratch, mask 0.75 both arms, img 96 / 200 epochs).
Because shrinking the *decoder* (unlike shrinking the *encoder*, the refuted e64/e48 capacity sweep) does not erode the
encoder's rank, this traces a sequence of full-rank craters of decreasing depth. At each step read the kNN crater,
`alignment_strong`, and RankMe frac. The *mask@0.042 point already in the P0.5.2 grid* (a full-rank crater from a
*different* lever on which the reader is quiet, 0.030) is the cross-lever anchor — no re-run needed to place it.

The catch the dose-down must respect: weakening the decoder shrinks the *crater* and the *decoder strength* together, so
neither a single point nor a bare "the fire vanished" is decisive. The *shape* of fire-vs-crater across the ladder is
what disentangles them.

## The verdict logic — read the shape, not a single point

- **Fire tracks the crater ⇒ quality reader.** As the decoder weakens, `alignment_strong` **declines in step with the
    crater**, crossing its ~0.05 bar around where the crater goes shallow. The reader responds to *how much quality was
    lost* — the deployable claim earned (toy-scale, partial; see the caveat). P0.5 gains a real reader deliverable.
- **Fire flat-then-cliff while a crater persists ⇒ reads the decoder (the shape that landed — see Results).** The fire
    stays flat (~0.06) then drops sharply to the null at some decoder-strength step, largely independent of the crater
    at that step — so a still-substantial crater (deeper than the mask lever's 0.042) sits with no fire. The fire is
    decoupled from quality; it reads the over-powered-decoder geometry. Confirmed by the mask anchor: a full-rank crater
    of 0.042 from a *different* lever is already quiet. The candidate is downgraded to a vehicle-specific detector, and
    the P0.5 ✅ rests on its two robust negatives (the geometry-suite trap + MAE resistance). This is what the P0.5.2
    flatness predicts.

## Results — the flat, decoupled shape (seed 0)

The dose-down landed the second shape, and more sharply than anticipated: the fire is *flat across the whole decoder
axis and fires even at negative crater*, so no matched-shallow-crater point was even needed.

| decoder (shortcut arm) | kNN crater | linear crater | RankMe frac | `alignment_strong` gap | fires (≥ 0.05)? |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| 64×2 (= healthy) | 0.000 | 0.000 | 1.00 | — | — |
| **128×4** | **−0.026** | **−0.004** | 1.17 | +0.055 | ✓ |
| **256×6** | **−0.008** | **−0.016** | 1.15 | +0.071 | ✓ |
| **384×8** | **+0.030** | **+0.030** | 1.06 | +0.078 | ✓ |
| 512×8 (P0.5.2 anchor) | +0.064 | +0.078 | 1.04 | +0.067 | ✓ |
| mask@0.042 (*different* lever, P0.5.2) | +0.042 | +0.016 | 0.93 | +0.030 | ✗ |

(Both crater columns are `healthy − shortcut`; **negative = the shortcut arm *beats* healthy** — no quality lost.
Healthy converged to kNN 0.376 in both new runs — bit-identical to P0.5.2, the reproducibility check.)

**Both ground-truth probes agree — the negative crater is not a kNN artifact.** P0.5.2 kept the linear *and* kNN probe
because they can diverge (the linear probe floors on weak reps, kNN reads local label-consistency at a lower bar), so if
`alignment_strong` were secretly tracking a real degradation the kNN merely missed, the linear probe would betray it
with a *positive* crater where kNN reads zero. It does not. The two probes are **concordant in sign at every decoder
step**, and at both firing-without-a-kNN-crater points the linear probe agrees there is no crater — indeed at 256×6 it
agrees *harder* (the shortcut arm is 0.016 better under the linear readout vs 0.008 under kNN). So the decisive
observation — `alignment_strong` firing where the shortcut arm is as good as or better than healthy — holds under *both*
label-based ground-truth readouts, not one. The kNN/linear divergence P0.5.2 flagged does not appear here: both probes
sit well above their floors (linear ≈ 0.48, kNN ≈ 0.38) and track each other.

`alignment_strong` fires the entire way down the decoder axis (+0.055 → +0.071 → +0.078 → +0.067), **flat with respect
to the crater** — which swings from −0.026 (the shortcut arm is *better* than healthy) to +0.064. It fires just as hard
when the shortcut arm has *no quality deficit at all* as when it craters, and it stays *silent* on the genuine +0.042
crater the *mask* lever produces. RankMe frac tracks the decoder the same way (1.17 → 1.04) — the geometry co-signature.
So the fire is coupled to decoder strength and fully decoupled from quality.

**Verdict: `alignment_strong` reads the over-powered-decoder *regime*, not representation quality — a confounder,
refuted as a quality reader.** The P0.5 mode's ✅ re-anchors on its two robust negatives (the P0.2 geometry suite is a
trap on MAE; MAE resists the shortcut, so the degradation is shallow). Artifacts:
[`artifacts/P0.5.3/`](artifacts/P0.5.3/) — the three dose-down `comparison.json`s (`dec{128x4,256x6,384x8}_seed0`).

## Insights

- **The reader is a *leading indicator of an over-powered decoder*, not of quality loss.** It fires from the first dose
    (128×4), where the shortcut probe actually *beats* healthy — well before any quality crater, which does not appear
    until 512×8. What the strong-augment alignment picks up is the encoder being freed to be *less
    augmentation-invariant* as the decoder grows; RankMe's mild inflation (1.17 → 1.04 down the axis) is the same
    decoder-induced geometry change, and quality does not follow it.
- **The dose-down is stronger evidence than the planned matched-depth comparison.** Fire-at-*negative*-crater rules out
    "reads quality" without needing a decoder crater engineered to the mask lever's depth — a shortcut arm that is
    *better* than healthy on the probe, yet flagged, cannot be a quality read.
- **This closes the P0.5 mode as the MAE half of one structural finding.** MAE trains pixel reconstruction, so
    representation quality is an emergent byproduct — and no internal MAE signal reads quality label-free: the loss
    decouples (the "loss lies"), the geometry readers mis-signal, and invariance (`alignment`) is not trained, so under
    a strong augment it reads the mechanism's geometry, not quality. The forgetting arc ([P0.3.9](P0.3.9.md)) hit the
    same wall from the other side (no trustworthy label-free forgetting reader). So label-free *quality* monitoring on
    an MAE backbone needs a labelled canary, or a backbone whose objective ties geometry to quality — a joint-embedding
    projector, the [P0.6.0](P0.6.0.md) test.

## The caveat that stays, whatever the outcome (the D3 confound)

The verdict is a *negative* — `alignment_strong` is not a quality reader — so the "strong-partial" hedge that would have
qualified a *positive* (a toy lever is not Phase-1's naturally-degraded MAE) is moot. What survives is the scope of the
negative: it is about MAE's *internal* signals at this toy backbone. This vehicle also cannot build the complementary
control — a healthy arm of *different geometry but equal quality* (a healthy over-powered-decoder arm) — because on MAE
the over-powered decoder *is* the mechanism of degradation, so geometry and quality are coupled on this lever; the
dose-down sidesteps that by firing at *negative* crater instead. Whether a naturally-degraded real-scale MAE has *any*
trustworthy quality reader remains the Phase-1 question.

## Scope (deliberately minimal — a seed-0 scout)

P0.5.3 is scoped to the one confound P0.5.2 left open: a **seed-0 decoder dose-down** (~3 intermediate decoder sizes
between 64×2 and 512×8), read against the P0.5.2 anchors (the 512×8 fire, the 64×2 zero, the mask@0.042 quiet). No mask
re-run (the anchor is already in the grid), no N-seed unless a ladder step lands ambiguously. Deliberately out of scope
(Phase-1, not new Phase-0 substudies): magnitude calibration (does the reader track *how much* quality was lost, beyond
the regime detection P0.5.2 found), permanence (the P0.3.7 severity≠permanence analogue), and any **new candidate
reader**. This keeps Phase-0 finite: settle whether the one candidate reads quality or the lever, then hand the deeper
questions to Phase-1.

## Relation to the carry-ins

- **C1 (the cross-vehicle / quality-vs-lever test) — this substudy is C1.** The audit named it the defining follow-up
    and deferred it to Phase-1 on an unreachability assumption; a fresh review showed the question is settleable at toy
    scale via a decoder dose-down (trace the fire against the crater), so it is promoted to this bounded Phase-0 run.
- **C2 (the one-reader portfolio) — the prerequisite question.** The ">1 instruments calibrated per mode" bar is met
    only loosely because `alignment_strong` is the lone label-free candidate. P0.5.3 resolves *whether that one reader
    is even a quality reader* before the portfolio question (a *second* MAE-native reader) is worth asking — that
    second-reader question stays Phase-1.
- **vs P0.5.2.** Same mode, same vehicle, same instrument; the new variable is a controlled **decoder-strength axis**,
    which turns P0.5.2's noisy across-seed hint (a shallow decoder crater firing) into a clean fire-vs-crater dose
    response.

## How to run

The harness is built and needs no change — the decoder lever is a CLI override on `scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py`
(`shortcut.decoder_dim` / `shortcut.decoder_depth`; both arms hold `shortcut.mask_ratio=0.75`, healthy keeps the 64×2
decoder). Gaudi usage per `docs/developing.md`. The seed-0 decoder dose-down:

```bash
# Decoder dose-DOWN at embed 96, from-scratch, competent scale, seed 0. Weaken the shortcut arm's
# decoder from the firing 512x8 toward the healthy 64x2; read the kNN crater, alignment_strong, and
# RankMe frac at each step, then trace the fire against the crater. Anchors already in the P0.5.2
# grid: 512x8 (crater 0.064, fires 0.067), 64x2 (== healthy, zero), mask@0.1 (crater 0.042, quiet 0.030).
for spec in "128 4" "256 6" "384 8"; do read dim depth <<< "$spec"
  ./scripts/run_gaudi_dev.sh -m /mnt gaudi-env-cafl4ds:latest 0 \
    python scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py device=hpu init=from_scratch \
    encoder.embed_dim=96 encoder.depth=6 encoder.num_heads=3 encoder.patch_size=8 \
    data=imagenette img_size=96 batch_size=128 epochs=200 eval_every_epochs=10 \
    max_per_class=null max_train_per_class=null \
    'shortcut.mask_ratio=0.75' "shortcut.decoder_dim=$dim" "shortcut.decoder_depth=$depth" seed=0
done
```

A fast, network-free CPU smoke (`device=cpu data=synthetic img_size=16` with small `epochs`, shortcut mask ≥ ~0.25) is a
wiring check only, not a scientific run. Status/verdict → [latest-results/P0.5.3](../latest-results/P0.5.3.md).
