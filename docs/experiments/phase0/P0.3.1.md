# Positive control — the forgetting operating point + the drift verdict (P0.3.1)

[P0.3.0](P0.3.0.md) delivered a *passing* two-sided forgetting gate on one distinct-task (vehicles/animals) corner, but
left two things open: the crater was modest and near the toy-scale noise floor, and the backbone-drift metrics were
demoted only because they were *inconclusive* at that scale. P0.3.1 sets out to figure out how to strengthen the signal
by magnifying the effect, and settle the drift verdict. The evidence redirected the study: rather than a deeper crater,
P0.3.1 maps the forgetting operating point — and finds P0.3.0's ~−0.07 crater is a *task/scale ceiling*, not an
under-tuned regime — while pinning down exactly what the drift metrics do and do not measure.

## The original hypothesis, and why the evidence overturned it

The plan was: scale the backbone up → larger *erodable delta* (`task_a_learned = R[0][0] − a_init`, the learned
representation above the random-init floor) → proportionally deeper crater. Two HPU runs refuted it:

- **Scale up, undertrained** (embed 384 / depth 12, 96px, 300 ep/phase): the bigger ViT learned *nothing*
    linearly-probeable on task A (delta **+0.003** vs P0.3.0's +0.096) — a larger model at a fixed horizon is simply
    undertrained (144 tokens at 96px and more parameters, same epochs).
- **Scale up, adequately trained** (384/12, 64px, 600 ep/phase): task A now learned **better** than P0.3.0 (delta
    **+0.110**) — yet the PC **barely forgot** (BWT **−0.008** vs −0.071). More capacity *buffers* against forgetting: a
    larger backbone has room to fit task B without overwriting task A.

The erodable delta and the crater moved in **opposite directions** with capacity, so scale is the wrong lever. This
pointed at **interference pressure** instead — tested with two parallel probes (one per HPU card): raise erosion (train
B longer) and lower capacity (tighten the representation so the two tasks compete for it).

## The regime map

All rows use the vehicles/animals split (task A = `{0,2,8,9}`, task B = `{1,3,5,6}`), 64px, linear probe; each is a
single-axis delta from the P0.3.0 baseline.

| regime | encoder (embed/depth) | epochs A/B | erodable Δ | PC crater (BWT) | healthy (BWT) | gate |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| **P0.3.0 baseline** | 256 / 8 | 300 / 300 | +0.096 | **−0.071** | −0.021 | ✅ |
| scale↑ (undertrained) | 384 / 12 (96px) | 300 / 300 | +0.003 | — | — | ❌ |
| scale↑ (trained) | 384 / 12 | 600 / 600 | +0.110 | −0.008 | +0.022 | ❌ |
| **erosion** (longer B) | 256 / 8 | 300 / **900** | +0.096 | **−0.067** | +0.012 | ✅ |
| **shrink** (less capacity) | **128 / 6** | 300 / 300 | +0.083 | −0.013 | +0.008 | ❌ |

Read down the **capacity axis** (trained, ~300/300): **128/6 → −0.013, 256/8 → −0.071, 384/12 → −0.008.** Forgetting is
**non-monotonic in capacity and peaks at the intermediate 256/8** — P0.3.0 already sits at the interference sweet spot.
Too large *buffers* (room to hold both tasks); too small learns features too generic/shallow to specialize-then-erode.
Read the **erosion axis**: tripling B-only training (300 → 900) moved the crater −0.071 → −0.067 — **flat**; the
representation reaches a stable B-specialized equilibrium (task-A retention ~0.55) within ~300 B-epochs.

## Findings

**⚠ Revised by [P0.3.2](P0.3.2.md):** this substudy's "passing" reads (including the erosion −0.067) were all
**single-seed (seed-0) probe-noise artifacts** — multi-seed + precise 200/200 probes show 0/5 seeds forget at this
corner. The operating-point map and drift verdict stand as toy-scale *characterisation*; the calibration gate itself
does not pass here. Real calibration escalates to CIFAR-100 ([P0.3.4](P0.3.4.md)).

- **The ~−0.07 crater is a task/scale ceiling, not a tuning deficiency.** Neither more capacity, less capacity, nor more
    erosion time deepens it; P0.3.0's modest crater is *inherent* to vehicles-vs-animals at STL-10 toy scale — and
    therefore trustworthy, not an artifact of an under-optimized regime.
- **Backward Transfer / Forgetting Measure are calibrated — two-sided, across the whole sweep.** They fire on genuine
    forgetting (PC BWT ≤ −0.067 in the intermediate regime) and the healthy replay control holds (|BWT| ≤ 0.03 in
    *every* run), and they read ≈ 0 where forgetting is genuinely absent. The gate "failures" (scale↑, shrink) are
    **true negatives** — forgetting really did not occur there — not missed detections. That the metrics track
    forgetting-magnitude correctly across a wide capacity/erosion space is the core calibration result.
- **Drift verdict.** With training-amount controlled — both erosion arms train 1200 epochs — **cka_drift is larger in
    the forgetting arm** (PC 0.059 vs healthy 0.038), the correct direction, matching P0.3.0 (0.051 vs 0.023). But the
    separation is only ~1.3–2×, it does not cleanly threshold, and it also reads PC > healthy in the *sub-threshold*
    shrink run (0.023 vs 0.014) — i.e. it partly tracks total respecialization, not forgetting specifically.
    **cosine_drift** is movement-dominated: larger in the replay arm in 3 of 4 runs regardless of whether forgetting
    occurred. Neither is gate-worthy — but the demotion is now **mechanistically explained** (drift ∝ backbone movement,
    only loosely aligned with forgetting), not the P0.3.0 "coin-flip."
- **MAE reconstruction** remains a *relative-retention* corroboration (the PC retains held-out task-A reconstruction
    worse than the replay arm) — reported, not gated.

## Why not a different class split

A deeper crater would need a more distinct A/B pair, but **STL-10 is bounded**: vehicles vs animals is the widest
low-level-structure gap the 10 classes afford, and already makes the most of the data available at this scale. Splitting
finer (within-vehicles, within-animals) *reduces* distinctness and would shrink the crater; no split raises the ceiling.
A materially larger crater is a **richer-data / larger-model question that belongs at Phase 1**, not a toy-scale knob.

## Instrument status

The transfer-probe forgetting instruments — per-era probe, Backward Transfer, Forgetting Measure — are **calibrated** at
toy scale: they fire on the induced forgetting event and stay quiet on the matched healthy control, with the fire/quiet
magnitudes (~−0.07 vs ±0.02) now characterised as a task-bounded ceiling rather than a tuning target. Backbone drift
(cka/cosine) is **not** a standalone forgetting detector here and is reported, not gated. The remaining rigor gap versus
the calibrated collapse mode is a **false-positive stress on the quiet side** (the P0.2.4 analogue) plus multi-seed
confirmation of the near-threshold fire — carried into the phase-0 index as the follow-on.

## How to run

Same harness/config as P0.3.0 (`scripts/positive_control_forgetting.py`,
`cafl4ds/configs/positive_control_forgetting.yaml`). The two interference probes run one per HPU card (see
`docs/developing.md`); the erosion run is the canonical passing artifact
([`artifacts/P0.3.1/comparison.json`](artifacts/P0.3.1/comparison.json)):

```bash
# erosion (card 0): P0.3.0 config, only epochs_b 300 -> 900
./scripts/run_gaudi_dev.sh -m /mnt/stl10 gaudi-env-cafl4ds:latest 0 \
    python scripts/positive_control_forgetting.py device=hpu \
    'task_a_classes=[0,2,8,9]' 'task_b_classes=[1,3,5,6]' \
    encoder.embed_dim=256 encoder.depth=8 encoder.num_heads=8 img_size=64 \
    ssl.decoder_dim=128 ssl.decoder_depth=4 ssl.decoder_heads=4 \
    epochs_a=300 epochs_b=900 batch_size=128 probe=linear recon_masks=16

# shrink (card 1): capacity below P0.3.0, same 300/300
./scripts/run_gaudi_dev.sh -m /mnt/stl10 gaudi-env-cafl4ds:latest 1 \
    python scripts/positive_control_forgetting.py device=hpu \
    'task_a_classes=[0,2,8,9]' 'task_b_classes=[1,3,5,6]' \
    encoder.embed_dim=128 encoder.depth=6 encoder.num_heads=4 img_size=64 \
    ssl.decoder_dim=64 ssl.decoder_depth=2 ssl.decoder_heads=4 \
    epochs_a=300 epochs_b=300 batch_size=128 probe=linear recon_masks=16
```

The go/no-go read is the gate summary's `PC BackwardTransfer` line against the healthy arm; the scale-up refutation is
reproduced by raising `encoder.embed_dim`/`encoder.depth` (and `img_size`) with a matched increase in `epochs_a`.
