# Positive control (representation-health gate) — the full-rank degradation crater & the candidate MAE-native readers (P0.5.2)

**What this substudy is — and what it is *for*.** The single successor to [P0.5.0](P0.5.0.md)/[P0.5.1](P0.5.1.md), and
the constructive close of the P0.5 mode (**shortcut / stable-loss representation degradation** — the failure MAE
*actually* exhibits, where the reconstruction loss reads healthy while representation quality is gone). Those two
substudies delivered the mode's *diagnostic* half: with two orthogonal levers they confirmed the **P0.2 suite has a
quality blind spot on an MAE backbone** — RankMe blind by construction, `uniformity` reading geometry not quality,
`alignment` negligible. What they could not do is *induce* the mode at toy scale: a from-scratch MAE at their modest
scale kept a useful representation, so the frozen probe barely craters, and a quality reader cannot be calibrated
without a real difference in quality to track. P0.5.2 removes that blocker — it produces a genuine degradation — and
then does the constructive work the mode was opened for: put candidate MAE-native quality readers on trial against it.

## The reframe — success is a *real, full-rank* degradation, not a near-chance crater

The gate this study inherited from P0.5.0/P0.5.1 asked the shortcut arm's probe to crater toward *chance* (a large
absolute gap). The runs showed that target was **mis-specified for what this mode is**, so we reframed it (the
load-bearing decision behind everything below):

- **Full-rank is the *defining* criterion, not a nicety.** This mode exists as a study distinct from P0.2 collapse
    precisely because RankMe (a cheap online signal) *stays high* while quality falls — that is the dissociation the
    mode is named for. If a lever buys a deeper crater by driving RankMe down, it has not induced *this* pathology
    harder; it has changed modes into collapse, which RankMe already catches. So keeping RankMe high matters *more* than
    crater depth — a crater bought with rank collapse is a leak into the mode we already instrument.
- **The pathology is the *dissociation*, not the severity.** The danger is a rep that degrades meaningfully *while every
    cheap signal (loss, RankMe) reads fine* — regardless of whether it bottoms out at chance. A real relative probe drop
    at preserved (even elevated) RankMe, with the shortcut arm's loss *lower* than healthy's, is the failure story
    itself.
- **The guardrail (so this isn't goalpost-moving).** Lowering the *severity* bar obliges us to *raise* the **reliability
    and specificity** bars, or we risk christening probe noise as a pathology. So the magnitude threshold comes down,
    but the degradation must be seed-robust (min-across-N, beyond the probe's run-to-run noise).

## The vehicle — as designed, and as it actually landed

The design opened on the *degrade-from-competent* path (a *pretrained* MAE continued under a shortcut regime), with a
capacity gate to control MAE's stickiness confound: a smaller competent model has less slack, so — the hypothesis — the
shortcut is forced to trade semantics away and the probe craters, where a large model has the spare capacity to keep
both and *resists*. The runs revised this:

- **Degrade-from-competent *resists* — the P0.3 stickiness, again.** Continuing a genuinely competent well (pretrained
    in-house on full Imagenette to kNN ≈ 0.39 / linear ≈ 0.53 at high RankMe) under the mask-shortcut regime does not
    erode it: the probe holds within noise of the pristine well at both a small (embed 96) and a larger (embed 192)
    capacity. A competent MAE is *sticky* — the same resistance the forgetting arc found. This confirms the confound the
    capacity gate was meant to control is real, and that a *gentle* lever cannot overcome it from competence.
- **So the crater comes from-scratch — the P0.5.0 design, revived by scale.** The reason P0.5.0/P0.5.1 reached for a
    pretrained well was that from-scratch at *their* toy scale gave the healthy arm no headroom. That blocker is gone:
    from-scratch at the now-competent scale (full Imagenette, img 96, 200 epochs) reaches healthy kNN ≈ 0.38 — real
    headroom — so the crater can be induced *from-scratch*, with the shortcut baked in from step 0, no stickiness to
    fight. From-scratch is therefore the crater vehicle; the pretrained-continue result is banked as the "MAE resists
    shortcut erosion from competence" finding.
- **Capacity is still the scout knob** — swept *down* (embed 192 → 96 → 64 → 48) to find where the shortcut bites
    hardest while the healthy arm at the same size still clears headroom.

Two arms, seed reset between them (the P0.2 `anti_collapse` / P0.3 `replay` / P0.5.0–P0.5.1 pattern): a **healthy** arm
(mask 0.75, lightweight decoder) and a **shortcut** arm differing by one lever. Read at the **encoder representation**
(mean-pooled patch tokens — MAE's only surface). **Multi-seed, verdict by min-across-N.**

### The levers, and what each does to the crater-vs-rank trade

- **Mask ratio** (low → interpolation shortcut, P0.5.0): the gentlest. From-scratch at mask 0.1 the encoder *still*
    learns useful features — the crater is small and RankMe stays high.
- **Over-powered decoder** (P0.5.1): the decoder does the semantic work, so the encoder can stay full-rank-but-useless —
    the sharpest test of the mode, and the one that produces the **largest degradation at *preserved* rank**. Because
    both arms sit at mask 0.75, it is also the **cleanest "loss lies"** (same task → apples-to-apples cross-arm loss)
    and it removes the mask-lever's attention-locality confound (below).
- **Combined** (low mask *and* over-powered decoder): deepens the crater further — but **at the cost of RankMe**, which
    starts to collapse. Under the reframe this is a *failure*, not a bigger success: it has drifted out of the full-rank
    regime toward P0.2 collapse.

## Instruments on trial

All at the encoder surface via the existing suite (`cafl4ds.measurements` + `HealthMonitor`), plus the new candidates:

- **Frozen-backbone probe (linear + kNN) — the ground truth.** Keep **both** (the P0.5.0/P0.5.1 discipline): the linear
    probe floors on weak reps while kNN registers local label-consistency at a lower threshold, so their divergence is
    itself diagnostic.
- **The candidate MAE-native readers — the deliverable, on trial.**
    - **Alignment under stronger augmentations** — the *same* `alignment` metric (mean embedding distance between two
        augmented views of one image; lower = more invariant), differing from the P0.5.0/P0.5.1 carry-in **only in the
        augment that forms the view pair**. **Light `alignment`** draws the pair from MAE's own mild spatial augment (the
        transform it applies before masking); under so gentle a perturbation a semantic rep and a low-level shortcut rep
        look *equally* invariant, so it reads noise. **`alignment_strong`** draws the pair from a heavier SSL-grade
        pipeline (random-resized-crop + flip + colour jitter + grayscale) — a strong but *semantic-preserving* transform —
        which is what exposes the gap: a semantic rep stays invariant to a change that preserves meaning, a shortcut rep
        keying on low-level detail does not. Read-only both ways (neither augment enters the MAE objective). **The current
        lead:** it is the one reader that fires two-sided on the full-rank decoder vehicle where RankMe is blind — but it
        fires *only* there (single-vehicle; see the cross-vehicle read in Findings), so it is a candidate quality reader,
        not yet a calibrated one.
    - **Unsupervised clusterability** — silhouette / k-means on the frozen rep, the most *direct* label-free proxy for the
        probe's class separability.
    - **Mean attention distance** — the patch-grid distance an attention head spans, from the ViT attention maps. Caveat
        learned in the scout: it is confounded by the mask lever, not a clean quality read — a low mask ratio makes heads
        local (inpaint from neighbours) *independent of quality*, and the sign even flips by regime (local on the
        small-well continue, global from-scratch). It is meaningful only on the decoder lever (both arms mask 0.75), where
        the attention-geometry confound is held fixed.
- **RankMe / effective rank — the geometry classifier and the mode's precondition** (see the reframe): high on a
    degraded probe = the clean full-rank-but-useless mode; a large drop = collapse-adjacent, out of scope.
- **The P0.2 quality readers (`alignment` light, `uniformity`) — the negative control.** P0.5.0/P0.5.1 predict they stay
    blind / mis-signal even on a genuine degradation.
- **Reconstruction loss — the anti-instrument.** Must not separate the arms in the alarming direction (the shortcut arm
    keeps a low, healthy-*looking* loss — indeed *lower* than healthy's on the decoder lever — while its probe falls).

The **warm-up-confound guard (the P0.3.9 lesson, for pretrained models).** For any *pretrained-continue* arm the
checkpoint is encoder-only, so a fresh decoder is spliced on and its early gradients perturb the encoder — a warm-up
transient that drops the probe at the *start* of the continue, on the same data. The harness now probes the **pristine
well pre-gradient** (a step −1 baseline, on a throwaway monitor) and reports whether the healthy arm **recovers** to it,
so a low healthy probe cannot be silently mistaken for warm-up damage vs. an incompetent well. (The from-scratch vehicle
has no such transient; the guard is inert there.)

## The reframed two-sided gate

At the operating point, N-seed, verdict by min-across-N:

1. **Full-rank precondition (primary).** RankMe preserved — shortcut/healthy RankMe ratio ≳ 0.85 (a large drop ⇒ this is
    collapse, out of scope). This is now the *defining* check, not a footnote.
1. **Loss does not alarm.** The shortcut arm keeps a low, healthy-looking loss (the "loss lies").
1. **Healthy headroom.** The healthy arm clears a real probe above chance (and, for a pretrained-continue arm, recovers
    to its pristine well baseline — the warm-up guard).
1. **Real, seed-robust degradation.** The shortcut arm's frozen probe (linear + kNN) is reliably below the healthy arm's
    across seeds (min-across-N beyond probe noise; a meaningful *relative* drop, not a near-chance absolute one).
1. **The verdict (the deliverable).** Does *any* candidate reader fire **two-sided** — fire against the degradation
    *and* stay quiet on a healthy-vs-healthy null — while the cheap panel (loss, RankMe) stays blind? A fire is a
    calibrated MAE-native quality reader (the portfolio gap closed); a clean null against a genuine degradation is a
    strong earned negative (proposals to Phase-1). *Which* reader is the headline. Magnitudes/thresholds are toy-scale;
    the fire/quiet verdict is the portable result.

## Findings

The wells pretrain to genuine competence, clearing the P0.5.0/P0.5.1 headroom wall (well_s embed 96: kNN 0.39 / linear
0.53; well_m embed 192: kNN 0.40 / linear 0.52; both high RankMe). Two arcs follow: the **crater hunt** (locate a
full-rank degradation) and the **N-seed verdict** (is it real, and does a reader track it).

### The crater hunt — lever × capacity (seed 0)

| vehicle | encoder | shortcut lever | healthy kNN | shortcut kNN | crater | RankMe ratio | full-rank? | reader |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| pretrained continue | e96 | mask 0.1 | 0.362 | 0.360 | 0.002 | 0.81 | ✓ | `attn_distance` (mask-confound) |
| pretrained continue | e192 | mask 0.1 | 0.388 | 0.352 | 0.036 | 0.93 | ✓ | none |
| from-scratch | e96 | mask 0.1 | 0.376 | 0.334 | 0.042 | 0.93 | ✓ | none |
| **from-scratch** | **e96** | **decoder 512×8** | **0.376** | **0.312** | **0.064** | **1.04** | **✓** | **`alignment_strong`** |
| from-scratch | e64 | decoder 512×8 | 0.366 | 0.300 | 0.066 | 0.84 | ~ | none |
| from-scratch | e48 | decoder 512×8 | 0.366 | 0.280 | 0.086 | 0.74 | ✗ | `uniformity` (geometry) |
| from-scratch | e96 | combined | 0.376 | 0.294 | 0.082 | 0.49 | ✗ collapsing | none |

Three structural findings, robust across the runs:

- **MAE resists the shortcut, both ways.** Continuing from competence barely moves (stickiness — the P0.3 lesson); and
    even *from-scratch* at mask 0.1 the encoder still learns a useful rep (only 0.04 below healthy). The mask lever is
    too gentle to induce the mode at this scale.
- **The full-rank corner is *shallow*, but real.** The over-powered decoder lever is the only one that degrades the
    probe while holding rank; the mask lever is too weak, and the *combined* lever deepens the crater (0.082) only by
    *collapsing the rank* (ratio 0.49) — i.e. by becoming P0.2 collapse. So a large useless-at-chance crater is not
    achievable in MAE at full rank — exactly why the near-chance target was the wrong bar.
- **The capacity gate is refuted.** Shrinking the encoder (e96 → e64 → e48) does not buy a deeper full-rank crater — the
    crater grows slightly (0.064 → 0.086) but only *by eroding RankMe* (1.04 → 0.84 → 0.74), the same crater-for-rank
    trade as the combined lever. `alignment_strong` fires only at the clean full-rank point (e96); where rank erodes it
    goes quiet and `uniformity` fires instead — reading the incipient rank drop (geometry), not quality. So embed 96 /
    decoder lever is the Pareto-best full-rank operating point.

**The reader fire is vehicle-specific, not crater-tracking (the cross-vehicle read).** Line the crater-hunt rows up by
`alignment_strong` gap and the "reads quality" story breaks: at *matched or greater* full-rank crater depth the reader
fires for the decoder lever and stays at the null for the others. The from-scratch **mask** crater (probe −0.042, RankMe
0.93 — full-rank) gives gap **0.030**, and the pretrained-continue **mask** crater (probe −0.036, RankMe 0.93) gives
**0.030** — both at the healthy-null level (~0.024), *not* a fire — while the shallower decoder crater (mean 0.052, only
0.028 on the weakest seed) *does* fire (0.063–0.083). Because the mask points are full-rank and still quiet, the
operative variable is the **lever**, not the rank: `alignment_strong` reads the over-powered-decoder geometry, not the
probe crater as such. A genuine quality reader should respond to a crater regardless of which lever produced it; this
one does not. That is the evidence base for the "candidate, not calibration" framing below, and why the defining
follow-up is a **cross-vehicle test** — does `alignment_strong` fire on a full-rank degradation from a lever *other
than* the over-powered decoder at matched probe depth? Deepening the mask/continue crater *up* to the decoder point's
0.064 is toy-unreachable (every attempt collapses the rank — the C3 ceiling below), which first read as "needs real
scale." But the match can be made the *other* direction — shrinking the **decoder** crater *down* to the mask lever's
0.042 full-rank depth, which keeps RankMe high — so the decisive comparison is toy-reachable after all. It is taken up
in [P0.5.3](P0.5.3.md) (the audit's C1, promoted from a Phase-1 hand-off to a bounded Phase-0 run).

### The N-seed verdict (5 seeds, embed 96, decoder lever)

|  | s0 | s1 | s2 | s3 | s4 | across-5 |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| probe crater (kNN) | +0.064 | +0.028 | +0.076 | +0.060 | +0.032 | 5/5 positive; **min +0.028**, mean +0.052 |
| RankMe ratio (S/H) | 1.04 | 1.04 | 1.03 | 1.03 | 0.97 | **5/5 full-rank** (min 0.97 ≥ 0.85) |
| loss lies (S floor < H) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | **5/5** (bad arm's loss is *lower*) |
| `alignment_strong` gap | +0.067 | +0.064 | +0.083 | +0.077 | +0.063 | **fires 5/5**, min +0.063, mean +0.071 |
| `uniformity` | mis | mis | mis | mis | mis | **mis-signals 5/5** (shortcut looks *more* uniform) |

The **dissociation *direction* is seed-robust (5/5)**: the cheap online panel reads healthy every seed (RankMe preserved
5/5; the loss is actually *lower* on the degraded arm 5/5), while the frozen probe is worse every seed (5/5 positive
crater). That is the "loss lies / RankMe lies" pathology the mode is named for, on a real, full-rank rep. The
*magnitude* is a separate, weaker claim: the kNN crater is min +0.028 / mean +0.052, so 2/5 seeds sit below the gate's
own `min_probe_gap = 0.05` line, and the harness `passed` flag is **True on only 2 of 5 seeds** (seeds 2, 3). So the
induction gate is a *means* — mostly sub-threshold here — and the mode verdict rests not on the gate but on the
seed-robust dissociation *direction* plus the reader verdict below. The headline is the direction, not "seed-robust
degradation" unqualified.

**The deliverable — `alignment_strong` fires two-sided, but as a detector of the *decoder-shortcut regime*, not (yet) a
calibrated quality reader.** It fires two-sided on all five seeds (+0.063 to +0.083); against the **healthy-vs-healthy
null** (pairwise gaps between the five healthy arms' values, top 0.024, σ 0.008) the smallest fire is **≈2.7× the
largest null gap (~8σ)** — that null-separation ratio is the portable statement, *not* the 0.05 fire threshold (a
reader-specific, toy-scale cutoff; `alignment_strong` would not clear the 0.20 bar its P0.2 siblings carry — max fire
0.083). It fires where the **entire P0.2 suite is blind or mis-signals**: `uniformity` fires *wrong-direction* every
seed (the shortcut rep looks *more* uniform — it reads geometry, not quality), `clusterability` likewise runs
wrong-direction, light `alignment` stays quiet, `attn_distance` is quiet and wrong-signed (and, on the mask lever,
confounded by attention geometry), and RankMe is blind by construction (the mode's precondition). **But the fire is
single-vehicle** (the cross-vehicle read above): it keys on the over-powered-decoder lever, not on the probe crater as
such — quiet on full-rank mask/continue craters of equal-or-greater depth, flat/reversed once rank erodes, and flat in
magnitude across a 2.7× crater swing. So it is a *promising MAE-native quality candidate*, calibrated here as a binary
detector of the decoder-shortcut regime, not an established quality reader; whether it tracks quality (vs the
decoder-lever geometry) is the defining cross-vehicle test, taken up in [P0.5.3](P0.5.3.md).

### Insights

- **MAE has a full-rank degradation mode, and it is shallow.** The pathology is *real* (a seed-robust dissociation) but
    *bounded*: at full rank the probe drop is modest (mean 0.05, min 0.03 — 2/5 seeds within ~noise of the ~0.024
    probe-floor), because pushing the shortcut harder collapses the rank into P0.2 territory. "Full-rank-but-*useless*"
    overstates it for MAE at this scale; "full-rank-but-*degraded*" is the honest name. This shallow ceiling is the
    mode's defining phenomenon-limit — the representation-degradation analogue of the forgetting mode's unreached
    genuine/permanent pole (P0.3 C2): whether a *competent real-scale* MAE has a *deeper* full-rank degradation, and
    whether such a degradation is *permanent*, are untouched here and are the open scaling questions.
- **`alignment_strong` reads the *regime*, not (yet) the *magnitude*.** Its fire is strikingly stable (+0.063–0.083)
    even as the probe crater swings 2.7× (0.028–0.076). So it is a reliable **binary detector** of the shortcut regime
    (fire ⇒ investigate this rep), but it does **not** track how much quality was lost — magnitude-calibration is a
    Phase-1 question, not something this vehicle settles. And the healthy-vs-healthy null it clears is only a
    *seed-noise* null: it certifies the fire is not run-to-run noise of one config (real, and worth having), but it does
    **not** control for a healthy arm of *different geometry but equal quality* (e.g. a healthy over-powered-decoder
    arm), so it cannot separate quality from the decoder-geometry fingerprint — the same cross-vehicle gap, seen from
    the null side.
- **Why a *stronger-augment* alignment works where light alignment fails.** MAE never optimizes invariance, so
    light-augment `alignment` reads noise (P0.5.0/P0.5.1). A *semantic* rep is nonetheless more invariant to a strong,
    meaning-preserving transform than a low-level shortcut rep — the strong augment is what exposes the difference, and
    it is the repurpose that rescues the reader.
- **The geometry readers mis-signal in a *consistent* direction.** `uniformity` and `clusterability` both say the
    shortcut rep is *healthier* (more uniform, more clusterable) while its probe is worse — a coherent warning that
    geometry-only readers can be actively misleading on MAE, not merely blind.

## Relation to the carry-ins

- **The deployable panel reduces to one reader (C2).** Of the label-free candidates only `alignment_strong` fires;
    RankMe is a precondition (blind by construction), `uniformity`/`clusterability`/`attn_distance` mis-signal or stay
    quiet, and the recon loss is the anti-instrument. So the [`index.md`](index.md) exit-gate's ">1 instruments
    calibrated per mode" is met only *loosely* — by counting the label-based frozen probe (ground truth, not a
    deployable reader) or the anti-instrument. As with the instability mode's lone grad-norm reader, a single-instrument
    calibration is acceptable when honestly scoped; whether a second MAE-native quality reader is needed is a Phase-1
    portfolio question, and it ties the C1 cross-vehicle test — whether the one reader is even a *quality* reader —
    which [P0.5.3](P0.5.3.md) resolves before the portfolio question is worth asking.
- **C3 (canary) relaxed.** The candidate readers are single-arm, current-stream — a firing quality reader deploys
    *without* a canary (unlike the forgetting readers, and like P0.5.0/P0.5.1).
- **C4 (backbone-agnostic, MAE prior).** MAE-native → ports under the MAE prior; the P0.2 collapse suite stays the
    conditionally-applicable projector leg, carried here only as the negative control it has earned.
- **vs P0.5.0/P0.5.1.** Same mode and same "does any reader read quality on MAE" deliverable; the change that unblocks
    calibration is *scale* (a from-scratch MAE that reaches competence, so the degradation has somewhere to fall) plus
    the reframed, full-rank-first success definition.

## How to run

The harness is built: `scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py` runs both arms (seed reset between them), takes
`init=from_scratch` (the crater vehicle) or `init=pretrained` (the degrade-from-competent probe, with the P0.3.9 warm-up
baseline guard), and reports the three candidate readers through `HealthMonitor` (`clusterability` + `attn_distance` new
in `cafl4ds.measurements`; `alignment_strong` reuses `alignment` under a heavier augment). Gaudi usage per
`docs/developing.md`. The crater vehicle (from-scratch, decoder lever, the operating-point candidate):

```bash
# From-scratch at competent scale, decoder-capacity lever (both arms mask 0.75; shortcut gets a big decoder).
# Capacity is the scout knob (embed_dim/depth); sweep it down to find the deepest full-rank degradation.
./scripts/run_gaudi_dev.sh -m /mnt gaudi-env-cafl4ds:latest 0 \
    python scripts/positive_control_shortcut.py device=hpu init=from_scratch \
    encoder.embed_dim=96 encoder.depth=6 encoder.num_heads=3 encoder.patch_size=8 \
    data=imagenette img_size=96 batch_size=128 epochs=200 eval_every_epochs=10 \
    max_per_class=null max_train_per_class=null \
    'shortcut.mask_ratio=0.75' 'shortcut.decoder_dim=512' 'shortcut.decoder_depth=8' seed=0
```

The degrade-from-competent probe (pretrained-continue) is a two-step workflow — pretrain the well with
`scripts/pretrain.py` (`max_train_per_class=null` for the full training set), then continue both arms from it at the
*same* encoder capacity with `init=pretrained pretrain_dir=<dir>`; it is retained as the "does the stronger lever crater
a *competent* rep?" control. A fast, network-free CPU smoke swaps `device=cpu data=synthetic img_size=16` with small
`epochs` — a wiring check, not a scientific run.

The N-seed verdict runs the operating-point config across `seed=0..4` (both arms each), plus a **healthy-vs-healthy
null** (two independent healthy runs) to calibrate the reader fire. Verdict by min-across-N.
