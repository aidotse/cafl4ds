# Candidate label-free lenses — layer-wise CKA + intrinsic dimension `[engineering + analysis]` (P1.1.2)

**What this substudy is.** The third P1.1 sub-study (see the [Phase 1 overview](index.md)). Phase 0 left the MAE
backbone with no label-free quality reader ([P0.5](../latest-results/P0.5.md)): every internal signal it tried either
decoupled from quality or read the lever that caused the damage. [P1.1.1](P1.1.1.md) then gave MAE its first
degradations of known size — surgical damage, injected mid-drive, at graded doses. P1.1.2 screens every candidate reader
against those branches: the new per-block lenses (CKA, Procrustes, RankMe and intrinsic dimension, read after each of
the 12 encoder blocks) and every scalar the corpus already logs.

**Verdict: of every lens and every logged signal, only CKA drift against the well survives the screen — as a detector of
change from a certified anchor, not as a quality reader.** It is the existing `cka_drift`, read on the backbone output.
The new lenses add no detection power over it, and all of Phase 0's MAE candidates fail again.

| Question | Answer |
| -- | -- |
| Does any label-free signal track MAE damage *and* ignore damage that costs nothing? | Yes, one. CKA drift fires on every seed the canary calls, ranks with the canary dent, and stays below even the smallest noise dose on re-initialization of the top 4 blocks — a large weight change at no canary cost. Every other reader that fires on the damage also fires on that control: it reads the weights, not the information. |
| Do Phase 0's MAE readers fare better on the real backbone? | No. RankMe, uniformity, the VICReg terms, loss and alignment fail again, now for a named reason: they miss the damage or read the lever. Three new candidates — Procrustes, intrinsic dimension (TwoNN) and cosine drift — fail the same way. |
| Do the per-block lenses add anything? | Not for detection. Per-block CKA calls the same arms as the backbone read. They do localize: MAE damage shows in every block, while the top-4 re-initialization stays in blocks 9–12, and barely registers there. On JE, the walk's compression sits in blocks 8–12 and the projector. |
| Is CKA drift ready for a monitor? | Not yet, for two reasons. It also fires on 3 of 5 healthy MAE walks and on every optimizer restart, with only about a 2× margin. And on the same ViT-B, Phase 0's natural MAE forgetting barely moved it (P0.3.6). |

**What CKA drift reads.** It measures how far the representation's *content* has moved from the well. It cannot say
whether a representation is good; it can only say the representation has left a state we certified as good. That is a
narrowing assumption Phase 0 never made: a competent, certified starting point. It is consistent with P0.5's structural
finding that no internal MAE signal reads quality label-free. It also explains the open problem: healthy adaptation
moves away from the well too.

The screen is necessary, not sufficient. Whether CKA drift can tell information loss from benign adaptation — read where
forgetting happens, against a diet-aware null — is [P1.1.3](P1.1.3.md)'s question. Certification on a natural MAE
failure stays with P1.3.

## Motivation

A reader for MAE quality needs two properties, and Phase 0 found no signal with both.

- **It must fire on information loss.** Here that means the damage arms the labelled canary calls (a linear dent of ~6–8
    points or more, [P1.1.1](P1.1.1.md#surgical-damage--the-canarys-power-curve)).
- **It must not fire on the lever.** In [P0.5.3](../latest-results/P0.5.3.md), `alignment_strong` looked like a quality
    reader until a dose-down showed it fired at decoder sizes with no quality deficit at all. It read the decoder, not
    the representation. Surgical damage has the same trap: across the noise ladder, weight change and canary dent rise
    together, so any "the weights moved" signal will look like a quality reader.

**The specificity control.** Re-initializing the top 4 blocks breaks that coupling. It changes the encoder's weights by
0.66 (relative L2 over all 12 blocks), between noise σ0.5 and σ0.75. Yet it costs the canary nothing on either backbone,
because the residual stream carries the lower blocks through. A reader that fires there reads the lever. It is the
dose-down analogue for surgical damage.

**Why the lenses.** The scalar instruments read one pooled embedding, so they say *that* the representation moved, not
*where*. Per-block CKA (Kornblith 2019) localizes drift. Procrustes is its recommended complement: CKA weights
directions by their variance, and Procrustes still sees change in low-variance directions (Ding et al. 2021). Intrinsic
dimension is the most reliable single label-free downstream proxy in a 260-model comparison (Arputharaj et al., TMLR
2026).

## Methodology

### What is read, on which checkpoints

Every read is label-free, on the drive's fixed probe set: 405 support + 405 query images (810 rows, above ViT-B's 768
features), identical across drives and the well.

- **Per-block features.** The patch-token mean after each of the 12 blocks, plus the monitor's surfaces (`backbone`, and
    `proj` on JE). Captured with forward hooks on the monitor's own forward
    ([`cafl4ds/block_features.py`](../../../cafl4ds/block_features.py)), so the final layer is exactly what the monitor
    reads.
- **Lenses, per layer** ([`cafl4ds/lenses.py`](../../../cafl4ds/lenses.py)): `cka_drift` (1 − linear CKA against the
    well), `dcka_drift` (the debiased estimator), `procrustes_drift` (against the well), `rankme`, and `twonn`
    (intrinsic dimension, read two-sided: it has no known harmful direction).
- **Scalars:** every label-free instrument the corpus logs at every step, loss and grad norm included. P1.1.1 left loss
    and grad norm out because they separate walk from IID mechanically. In a twin read that confound cancels: a damaged
    arm and its twin see identical batches.
- **Checkpoints** — all saved earlier, so the screen needed no new drives:
    - the well (`wells/p110_deep/<bb>_o0.0.pt`), every lens's anchor;
    - P1.1.0's overlap-0 drives, walk and IID × seeds 0–4, at their 18 leg checkpoints;
    - each P1.1.1 damage branch × seeds 0–2: its end state, and the state right after the damage, rebuilt from the step-65
        checkpoint and the run's own damage config. The rebuild reproduces the recorded post-damage RankMe, CKA drift and
        cosine drift exactly.

### The read

- **Null bands.** Each lens is calibrated with P1.1.1's machinery
    ([`reading_protocol.py`](../../../cafl4ds/reading_protocol.py)) on the 5 healthy IID drives at their 18 checkpoints.
    Each lens is one *panel*, all its layers sharing a threshold (so "any layer fired" holds α = 0.05). Each scalar is a
    panel of its own, calibrated on P1.1.1's 10 IID seeds at every step.
- **Twin effect.** Each damaged arm × seed is read against its twin, in units of the null's seed spread at that
    checkpoint, signed so positive means harm ([`lenses.twin_effect`](../../../cafl4ds/lenses.py)). Two reads:
    **immediate** (just after the damage against just before, no training between) and **end** (the branch's last state
    against the dose-0 branch's).
- **The three bars.** *Sensitive*: fires on the arms the canary called on all 3 seeds. *Specific*: stays quiet on
    re-init 4, and ranks re-init 4 below the noise doses. *Quiet*: stays quiet on healthy drives.

## Findings

### MAE: CKA drift is the only reader that is both sensitive and specific

18 seeds were canary-called (σ0.75, σ1.0, re-init 12, on both orders); 6 were re-init 4. "Below" counts the noise doses
whose median harm re-init 4 stays under (4 = below the smallest).

| Reader | Immediate: fires on called / quiet on re-init 4 / ρ canary | End: same | Re-init 4 below… (imm / end) |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| `cka_drift` (backbone) | 18/18 · 6/6 · +0.82 | 17/18 · 5/6 · +0.78 | all 4 / 3 |
| `cka_drift@*` (per block) | 18/18 · 6/6 · +0.91 | 12/18 · 6/6 · +0.82 | all 4 / all 4 |
| `procrustes_drift@*` | 18/18 · **0/6** · +0.87 | 18/18 · 2/6 · +0.84 | 3 / 3 |
| `twonn@*` | 18/18 · **0/6** · +0.60 | 18/18 · 6/6 · +0.81 | 3 / 3 |
| `cosine_drift` | 18/18 · **0/6** · +0.71 | 18/18 · **0/6** · +0.74 | 2 / 2 |
| `alignment` | 18/18 · 2/6 · +0.69 | 18/18 · 6/6 · +0.79 | 2 / all 4 |
| `uniformity`, `mean_feature_var`, `offdiag_cov` | 13–18/18 · **0/6** | 12–15/18 · 2–3/6 | 0 / 2–3 |
| `rankme` (backbone or per block) | 6/18 | 10–16/18 | — (non-monotone in dose) |
| `loss` (end only) | — | 12/18 · 6/6 · +0.83 | — / 3 |
| `grad_norm` (end only) | — | 0/18 | — |

What the table says:

- **Five readers fall into the P0.5.3 trap.** Procrustes, intrinsic dimension, cosine drift, uniformity and the VICReg
    terms all fire on re-init 4 at once. On their ladders it sits above σ0.3 or σ0.5 — and for uniformity and the VICReg
    terms, above every noise dose. They read the perturbation.
- **Only CKA drift passes both reads.** On re-init 4 it stays below even σ0.3 (immediate backbone *z* 0.2–2.9, against
    threshold 3.83). Across the noise ladder it rises with the canary dent.
- **CKA drift sees early what the canary misses.** Its three fires on canary-"quiet" arms are all σ0.5 IID seeds. Those
    carried immediate canary dents of 3–7 points that the canary's rule missed. So these are early detections, not false
    alarms.
- **Why Procrustes fails where CKA passes.** It is the property Procrustes was chosen for: it weights low-variance
    directions. Re-init 4 perturbs those directions of the output, and the canary-relevant information is not in them.
- **Debiasing changes nothing here.** `dcka_drift` equals `cka_drift` to the third decimal. With 810 rows against 768
    features, and drifts this small, the high-dimension bias is negligible.
- **Alignment and loss are partial.** Each passes specificity on the end read only. A monitor sees the immediate state
    too, where alignment fires on re-init 4. Loss is a twin-only reader (next section).
- **Rank agreement is weaker evidence than the control.** The ρ values rank against the canary dent, but weight change
    and canary dent are themselves correlated across doses (the drift readers' ρ against weight change is +0.7 to
    +0.96). The decisive evidence is re-init 4: one arm, 6 seeds.

### Phase 0's MAE readers, re-tested on the real backbone

Most of the readers screened here are not new. Phase 0 already put them on trial as MAE quality readers
([P0.5](../latest-results/P0.5.md)), on a from-scratch tiny ViT and a shortcut degradation. P1.1.2 re-tests them on the
pretrained ViT-B, against damage of known size and a specificity control. They fail again, and now for a named reason.

| Reader | Phase 0's verdict on MAE | P1.1.2, against the damage branches |
| -- | -- | -- |
| RankMe | blind by construction (P0.5.0–P0.5.2) | insensitive: rises, then falls with dose (6/18 immediate) |
| Uniformity | mis-signals: calls the degraded rep healthier (P0.5.2) | reads the lever: fires on re-init 4 (0/6 quiet) |
| VICReg variance / covariance | part of the P0.2 suite that P0.5 called a trap on MAE | reads the lever (0/6 quiet) |
| Loss | "the loss lies": decouples from quality (P0.5.2) | twin-only: tracks damage at the end, but fires on 4/5 healthy walks |
| Alignment | its strong-augment variant was the one candidate, refuted as a lever reader (P0.5.3) | reads the lever on the immediate read (2/6 quiet) |
| Procrustes, intrinsic dimension, cosine drift | not tried | read the lever (0/6 quiet) |
| **CKA drift** | read as a *forgetting* reader (P0.3.0, P0.3.6, P0.3.8, P0.3.10): the right direction, but tiny and not separable from a healthy arm that also trains | **passes: sensitive and specific** |

Two Phase-0 readers are not re-tested. Clusterability and mean attention distance are not logged in this corpus.

**The Phase-0 record on CKA drift tempers its pass here.** [P0.3.6](../phase0/P0.3.6.md) ran on the same pretrained
ViT-B MAE. Its natural forgetting cost the probe ~6 points (BWT −0.062), about the size of the dents the canary calls
here. Yet backbone CKA drift moved only ~0.004–0.013, and the healthy replay arm sometimes drifted more. Surgical damage
moves the representation's content a lot; that natural forgetting barely moved it. So the pass here shows that CKA drift
sees damage that changes the representation's content, not yet that it sees natural information loss.

### MAE: CKA drift is not yet quiet on benign movement

The damage read is twin-relative. A deployed monitor has no twin: it reads one drive against the IID bands. Here is
`cka_drift` read that way, on movement that costs no information:

| Trajectory (no information lost) | Fires | Max *z* per seed (threshold 3.83) |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Fresh healthy IID seeds (10–14) | 0/5 | 2.2–3.5 |
| Healthy walk (P1.1.0, seeds 0–4) | **3/5** | 2.8–5.3 |
| Optimizer restart (dose-0 twins, IID / walk) | **3/3 · 3/3** | 4.2–7.2 |

Against that, the canary-called damage reads at immediate *z* 6.2–9.9 at σ0.75 (medians ~9–10 at σ1.0 and re-init 12).
So the margin over the healthy walk is about 2×, and an optimizer restart overlaps it.

- **The walk's drift is benign.** P1.1.0 found the MAE walk ≈ IID, and the canary never fires on it.
- **The restart is a branching artefact, but not only that.** Resetting AdamW mid-drive jolts drift away from the well.
    A deployment that resets optimizer state — a federated round, for instance — would meet the same jolt.
- **End effects are smaller than immediate ones.** At the end of the drive, CKA drift has partly come back toward the
    twin (σ0.75 end *z* 3.5–9.9), while the canary dent persists. So CKA drift partly heals where the information loss
    does not.

The rule P1.1.1 applies already includes `cka_drift` in MAE's label-free panel. This is why that panel fires on 4 of 5
MAE walk seeds ([P1.1.1](P1.1.1.md#read-on-the-walk)).

### Localization: where damage and compression live

**MAE damage is everywhere; the specificity control is not.** Per-block CKA drift, immediate harm *z*, median over 6
seeds (panel threshold 5.95):

| Dose | blocks 1–4 | blocks 5–8 | blocks 9–12 | backbone |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| noise σ0.5 | 2.9–6.1 | 4.9–6.3 | 6.5–6.8 | 6.0 |
| noise σ0.75 | 5.5–10.3 | 8.4–10.3 | 9.5–10.2 | 9.5 |
| re-init 12 | 7.9–12.0 | 9.7–11.8 | 10.2–11.2 | 8.9 |
| re-init 4 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0.3–2.8 | 2.2 |

Noise damage shows from block 1 up, with block 2 consistently the least affected. Re-init 4 moves only the top blocks,
and only slightly. So per-block CKA locates damage correctly, but the backbone read already carries the call.

**JE's walk compression sits in the top third and the projector.** Walk-vs-IID effect size (Cohen's *d*, 5 vs 5 seeds)
at the end of the daytime block, then of the drive:

| Lens | blocks 1–7 | blocks 8–12 | backbone | projector |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| debiased CKA drift, daytime end | 0.0–3.2 | 3.6–5.3 | 3.0 | 8.1 |
| Procrustes drift, daytime end | 0.7–2.7 | 4.0–5.6 | 3.3 | 10.0 |
| debiased CKA drift, drive end | −0.4–2.6 | 1.7–3.5 | 1.7 | 2.7 |
| TwoNN dimension, drive end | −0.7–1.7 | −0.4 to **−5.8** (block 12) | −2.4 | −2.5 |

The drift lenses fire on every walk seed — always at the backbone, and at some of blocks 7–12. Intrinsic dimension fires
only at block 12 (4 of 5 seeds), where the walk lowers it. Most of the drift recedes by the drive's end, consistent with
P1.1.1's finding that the compression reverts. The picture is the tunnel effect's (Masarczyk 2023): the late blocks
specialize, and the early blocks hold. On MAE, no lens separates walk from IID (|*d*| ≤ 1.4 everywhere; 0 of 5 walk
seeds fire).

### JE damage: the readers that see it also fire on the walk

JE's canary called 24 seeds (σ0.5 and up, re-init 12); 6 were re-init 4. Backbone `uniformity` passes the damage screen
(immediate 24/24 · 6/6 · ρ +0.87). Procrustes per block nearly does (23/24 · 5/6). Intrinsic dimension and the projector
VICReg terms fail specificity (0/6). Backbone CKA drift is nearly blind to JE damage (5/24 immediate).

None of this yields a JE reader. Nearly every JE label-free scalar fires on the healthy walk (5 of 5 seeds for 11 of 15,
backbone uniformity at *z* up to 8.9), because the walk's benign compression moves them. Telling JE damage from JE
compression label-free is P1.4's question, where backbone RankMe is the standing candidate (P1.1.1).

## Insights

- **The survivor is a change detector, and its anchor is the assumption.** CKA drift passes because the well is
    certified competent, so "far from the well" means "far from good". On a from-scratch model there is no such anchor.
    Phase 0's MAE degradations were mostly of that kind (P0.5's shortcut arm trained from scratch), so the reader could
    not even be posed there. P0.3.6 is the exception: it had the anchor, and pooled drift was too faint (above).
- **A specificity control is cheap and decisive.** One arm with a large weight change and no information loss sorted
    eleven candidate readers. Five that looked sensitive turned out to read the lever. Any future screen with surgical
    damage should carry such an arm.
- **The information sits in the high-variance directions of the output.** CKA weights by variance and passes. Procrustes
    weights every direction equally and fails. For a downstream canary that is a 3-class linear read, this is
    unsurprising — but it means a "more sensitive" similarity metric is not a better quality reader.
- **Intrinsic dimension does not transfer as a quality proxy here.** It is reported as the most reliable proxy *across*
    models. Within one model's trajectory, it reads weight noise, which spreads variance across directions.

## Code validity

- [`measurements.py`](../../../cafl4ds/measurements.py) gains `twonn_id`, `debiased_linear_cka` and
    `procrustes_distance`. Each is tested on known answers
    ([`test_measurements.py`](../../../tests/unit/test_measurements.py)): a 5-dimensional subspace reads TwoNN ≈ 5;
    unrelated wide matrices read debiased CKA ≈ 0 where plain CKA reads > 0.5; two 1-D vectors read Procrustes = 2 −
    2|ρ|; a change confined to low-variance directions moves Procrustes and not CKA. Two are checked against independent
    implementations to 1e-6: TwoNN against `skdim.id.TwoNN`, and debiased CKA against Kornblith's feature-space formula.
- [`block_features.py`](../../../cafl4ds/block_features.py) — the block reads equal a hand-rolled forward, the surfaces
    equal the monitor's, batching does not change them, and the hooks are removed
    ([`test_block_features.py`](../../../tests/unit/test_block_features.py)).
- [`scripts/extract_block_features.py`](../../../scripts/extract_block_features.py) — end to end on the synthetic
    source, the rebuilt post-damage state reproduces the branch's recorded RankMe and weight change
    ([`test_scripts_e2e.py`](../../../tests/unit/test_scripts_e2e.py)). On HPU it reproduces the recorded BDD readings
    exactly (spot-checked on `mae_iid_noise1.0`, 3 seeds, post-damage and end).
- [`lenses.py`](../../../cafl4ds/lenses.py) and the screen's bookkeeping are unit-tested
    ([`test_lenses.py`](../../../tests/unit/test_lenses.py),
    [`test_analyze_lenses.py`](../../../tests/unit/test_analyze_lenses.py)). The reading protocol's `direction` now
    strips a `@<layer>` suffix, so a per-layer lens inherits its base's harmful direction.
- `scikit-dimension` (and pandas, which it imports undeclared) join the **dev** dependencies only; the package does not
    import them.

## How to run

On the Gaudi node, from the repo root. The extraction needs the P1.1.0 leg checkpoints and the P1.1.1 branch run dirs
([P1.1.1 artifacts](P1.1.1.md#artifacts)).

```bash
# Per-block features from every saved checkpoint, JE on card 0 and MAE on card 1 (~15 min).
./docs/experiments/phase1/artifacts/P1.1.2/queue.sh

# The screen (CPU, ~4 min the first time; seconds once lens_values.json is cached) — writes outputs/p112/lens_report.json.
uv run python scripts/analyze_lenses.py

# Or from the promoted lens values alone:
uv run python scripts/analyze_lenses.py "features='docs/experiments/phase1/artifacts/P1.1.2/features_{bb}'"
```

## Handover to P1.1.3, P1.3 and P1.4

- **P1.1.3 (the CKA drift deep dive).** Does CKA drift tell information loss from benign adaptation? Its leads: drift
    read only on the regimes the walk is *not* streaming (where forgetting lives, and the read pooled drift lacked in
    P0.3.6); a null calibrated on healthy walks as well as IID drives; how long the optimizer-restart jolt lasts; and
    JE's lr-1e-4 walk as a natural, label-confirmed degradation on BDD. The per-block inline calibration this sub-study
    first planned is dropped: per-block CKA adds no detection on MAE, and the backbone read is already logged and
    calibrated on 15 healthy seeds.
- **P1.3.** CKA drift is usable now as a *comparative* reader: one arm against a matched control, which is how P1.3
    reads legs (b) and (c). It is not yet a single-drive alarm. Log it beside the fine-tune probe; a natural MAE failure
    is its certification.
- **P1.4.** Re-run the collapse dial with `checkpoint_legs=final` (α ∈ {0, 1} × 3 seeds on IID, lr 1e-4, ~1 card-hour)
    and read whether collapse shows in the same late blocks as the walk's compression, beside the stop-gradient knob's
    extra seeds.
- **P1.6.** An optimizer restart jolts CKA drift (*z* 4–7 on the dose-0 twins). A federated design that resets optimizer
    state each round will meet it.
