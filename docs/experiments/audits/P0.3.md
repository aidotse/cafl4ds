# Audit — P0.3.x (forgetting-mode instrument calibration)

Peer-review-style audit of the forgetting failure-mode sub-studies (P0.3.0 → … → P0.3.8), conducted 2026-08-10 against
the substudy docs, their `latest-results` distillates, and the promoted artifacts under
[`../phase0/artifacts/`](../phase0/artifacts/) (`P0.3.0`, `P0.3.1`, `P0.3.4`, `P0.3.5`, `P0.3.6`, `P0.3.7`, `P0.3.8`).
Numbers were spot-checked against the JSON gates and reproduce (the P0.3.6 dose-response, the P0.3.4 N=5 fire, the
P0.3.5 kNN/recovery reads, and the P0.3.8 arms were all re-read from the artifacts); this is a review of *conclusions*,
not arithmetic.

Each point carries a **→ Remediation** line tagging how it should be addressed: `[wording]` (doc edit, no runs),
`[analysis]` (post-hoc on existing data, no new runs), `[new runs]` (re-run an existing config), `[new substudy]`,
`[decision]` (needs an architectural call), or `[defer → Phase-1]`.

## Verdict

As a *campaign*, P0.3 is the most honest thread in Phase 0: it refuted its own toy-scale fire under multi-seed scrutiny
(P0.3.2), exhausted every toy lever before escalating (P0.3.1–P0.3.3), separated the supervised sanity-check from the
real target (P0.3.4), and audited the genuine-vs-apparent and permanent-vs-recoverable axes with real care (P0.3.5). The
`index.md` status cell is already unusually candid — it says outright that CKA does not transfer, that the recon reader
false-fires on output shifts, and that the *genuine* (permanent) pole is unreached.

But the ✅ on the forgetting **mode** over-reads in three specific, load-bearing ways that the docs do *not* flag:

1. The mode-flipping fire (P0.3.6, the label-free SSL-MAE fire) is **single-seed, single-LR, and shallow** — and P0.3.2
    is the campaign's own proof that a single-seed forgetting fire brushing the −0.05 bar is a probe-noise draw that
    must be shown seed-stable. The ✅ rests on **n = 1** by the campaign's own standard (A2/B1).
1. The one label-free reader that survives (the MAE recon gap) is **not a graded forgetting signal** — it is essentially
    a replay-exposure differential, roughly constant across the whole dose-response and driven by the *healthy* arm's
    recon improvement, not PC degradation (A4). CKA doesn't transfer; BWT/FM need labels the label-free stream lacks.
    So the *deployment-relevant* calibration reduces to one coarse, replay-dependent reader with a known output-shift
    false positive (A1) — structurally the same collapse-to-one-detector the collapse-mode audit found (P0.2 A1).
1. The **genuine (deep/permanent) MAE pole is uncalibrated** — every MAE fire available is shallow and ~100%
    recoverable, the savings guard was never exercised, and the only permanence evidence (a knee) is on the
    *supervised* fire, which is not the target (C2).

Grouped below as over-claims (A), contradictions/tensions (B), structural gaps (C), and conclusions the data supports
but the docs did not draw (D).

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- **The self-refutation (P0.3.2) is exemplary.** Discovering that the P0.3.0/P0.3.1 "passes" were single-seed
    probe-noise draws — and re-scoping rather than discarding them — is exactly the discipline most work skips. It also
    produced a reusable quantitative result (a 4-way forgetting BWT needs ~200/200-scale probes).
- **The supervised/label-free split is drawn cleanly.** P0.3.4 is explicitly framed as a *necessary-not-sufficient*
    upper-limit sanity check, not the calibration; the mode was correctly held at 🟡 until an MAE label-free fire existed
    (P0.3.6).
- **The genuine-vs-recoverable audit (P0.3.5) is a genuine contribution.** The kNN-craters-deeper result (the refit
    linear BWT is the *conservative* reader) and the matched-LR permanence knee are both non-obvious and well argued.
- **The recon false-positive (P0.3.8 arm G) is caught and mechanistically explained** — a decoder-output shift, not
    representation forgetting — and the two-sided replay control is correctly identified as what exposes it.
- **The `index.md` cell already carries the hard caveats** (CKA non-transfer, recon FP, scoped resistance, unreached
    genuine pole). The audit's job is the three over-reads it does *not* yet carry.

## A. Over-claims / conclusions stronger than the evidence

**A1 — The *deployment-relevant* (label-free) calibration reduces to a single, replay-dependent reader.** The mode ✅ is
framed as "instrument + MAE, label-free." Sort the readers by what deployment actually has:

- **BWT / FM** — the "reliable readers" — need a dense **labelled, held-out per-era probe** (60–200 images/class). The
    project premise is a *label-free* transient stream; the P0.3.6 framing itself calls the label-free monitors "the
    only signals available in deployment." So the reliable readers are, by the project's own definition, *not* the
    deployable ones.
- **`cka_drift`** — does **not** transfer from the supervised fire to the MAE fire (P0.3.6, P0.3.8): tiny (~0.004) and
    not reliably ordered.
- **MAE recon gap** — the sole surviving label-free reader — and it is weak (A4) and needs a two-sided replay control it
    cannot have single-arm (C3).

So the label-free forgetting calibration is *one* coarse reader, replay-dependent, with a documented output-shift false
positive (P0.3.8). That is far more precarious than "✅ calibrated in the target regime" conveys — it is the exact
collapse-to-one-detector shape the collapse audit flagged (P0.2 A1).

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]`. State plainly in the distillate and the `index.md` cell that the
label-free deployment reader reduces to the recon gap; carry BWT/FM as "available only if the loop maintains a labelled
canary set" (ties C3).

**A2 — The mode-flipping fire is single-seed, single-LR, and shallow — unreplicated by the campaign's own standard.**
P0.3.6, P0.3.7, and P0.3.8 are **all seed 0** (confirmed: every promoted artifact is `*_seed0`). The fire passes at
**exactly one LR** (`3e-4`, PC BWT −0.062 — i.e. 0.012 past the −0.05 bar); the adjacent `4e-4` is a −0.048 near-miss
*non-fire* and `2e-4`/`1e-4` do not fire. This is precisely the configuration P0.3.2 spent an entire substudy proving
untrustworthy: a single-seed forgetting fire brushing the −0.05 threshold is a favorable probe-noise draw until shown
seed-stable (min-across-N). The ✅ that flips the whole mode therefore rests on **n = 1**, at a single operating point,
by the campaign's own hard-won yardstick.

→ **Remediation:** `[new runs]` — **highest priority (C1).** Re-run the P0.3.6 operating point at N = 5 seeds and report
min-across-seeds PC BWT vs −0.05 and max healthy |BWT| vs 0.03, exactly as P0.3.2/P0.3.4 do. This is a small, bounded,
directly-✅-enabling run; until it exists the mode ✅ is one seed.

**A3 — "Monotone in LR / strengthens through 4e-4" is contradicted by the artifact.** The distillate and P0.3.6 call the
dose-response "monotone in LR … emerges at 2e-4 and strengthens through 4e-4." The gates say otherwise — PC BWT is
**+0.012 → −0.020 → −0.062 → −0.048** across `{1e-4, 2e-4, 3e-4, 4e-4}`: non-monotone, deepest at 3e-4, *shallower and
failing* at 4e-4. Only one LR fires. Moreover the crater depth tracks R00 (well depth: 0.645 / 0.698 / **0.707** /
0.673), and P0.3.6 itself notes R00 wanders 0.65–0.71 run-to-run from the fresh-decoder warming transient — so the
single firing LR **coincides with the deepest-well draw**, and part of the "LR dose-response" is a well-depth
dose-response the run does not control for. The honest description is "a single firing LR flanked by non-fires, with
crater depth confounded by R00 variance," not "a tunable, monotone phenomenon."

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + ties D1/A2. Correct the monotonicity claim; the freeze-encoder variant (D1) plus
seed/LR replication (A2) would disentangle LR from R00.

**A4 — The recon gap "corroborates" but does not track forgetting; it is a replay-exposure differential.** Across the
full dose-response the recon gap is essentially **flat (+0.030 → +0.032 → +0.036 → +0.032)** while the crater swings
from +0.012 (no forgetting — the PC probe *improves*) to −0.062. It "corroborates" (> 0.03) at `2e-4` where the probe
does **not** fire (−0.020), and at the fire the PC arm's own recon barely moves (`pc_recon_a_rise` +0.004); the gap is
carried almost entirely by the **healthy** arm's recon *improvement* from extra task-A replay (−0.032). So the "+0.036
recon gap" mostly measures "the replay arm got more task-A gradient steps" — a near-tautology — not "the PC forgot in
reconstruction terms," and it does not scale with crater depth. Crowning it "the trustworthy label-free reader" over-
reads: it is at best a coarse threshold indicator, it cannot exist single-arm (there is no healthy arm in deployment),
and P0.3.8 shows it blows up 10× on a cosmetic output shift. Flat-and-tautological on genuine forgetting, explosive on
output shifts is close to the opposite of trustworthy.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + `[analysis]`. Downgrade the recon reader from "trustworthy label-free reader" to "a
replay-exposure differential that is a coarse presence/absence indicator only, requires a two-sided control, and
over-fires on output shifts." The `pc_recon_a_rise ≈ 0` decomposition is already in the artifacts — surface it.

## B. Contradictions / internal tensions

**B1 — P0.3.6 *defines* "genuine" to require seed-stability, then runs one seed.** The doc's own note: *"genuine crater
… means a real detection — a tunable, two-sided, **seed-stable** signal."* The runs vary neither seed nor (meaningfully)
LR. So "genuine," by the substudy's own definition, is asserted but not demonstrated — the seed-stable clause is unmet.
This is the definitional half of A2.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` now, `[new runs]` to actually satisfy it (A2/C1). Either replicate, or soften "genuine"
to "a single-seed two-sided fire, pending seed-stability."

**B2 — The stated deployment "tell" for the recon false positive is contradicted by its own data.** P0.3.8 gives the
heuristic *"a large recon gap **with a quiet probe**/savings is the tell"* for the grayscale output-shift FP. But in
that very arm the probe BWT is **−0.065 — a genuine fire, the same depth as the color baseline (−0.062)**: the probe is
*not* quiet, and the representation did *not* "shrug off" grayscale (it forgot as much as color). The real FP is that
recon over-reads the *magnitude* (10×) of an otherwise-baseline-depth fire — the correct tell is "recon gap ≫ the
probe-implied crater depth," not "quiet probe." As written, the heuristic would miss the case it was derived from.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Fix the tell to "recon gap disproportionate to the probe crater," and drop the
"encoder/probe shrugs it off" characterization — grayscale craters the probe identically to color.

**B3 — "MAE resists catastrophic forgetting" and "here is the MAE forgetting fire" sit in tension, and the ✅ leans on
the fragile exception.** The campaign's headline architectural finding is resistance; the single counterexample (P0.3.6)
required a *pretrained ImageNet* well + a *cross-dataset* Imagenette→CIFAR split + *matched-distribution replay* + one
favorable seed/LR/R00 draw to produce a −0.062 crater that is ~100% recoverable. That is arguably the exception that
proves the resistance rule — yet the mode ✅ depends on it entirely. The docs present resistance and the fire as
complementary; they are closer to "MAE essentially does not forget in-regime, and the one contrived setup that nudges it
is shallow, single-seed, and recoverable."

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Frame the ✅ as "a two-sided MAE fire *exists* and BWT/FM read it," explicitly narrow,
rather than "the forgetting mode is calibrated."

## C. Structural gaps to close before Phase 1

**C1 — [Highest] Seed-replicate and de-confound the MAE fire.** The ✅'s entire foundation is one seed at one LR (A2, A3,
B1). Re-run P0.3.6's `lr=3e-4` operating point at N = 5 seeds; ideally also add the freeze-encoder phase-A variant (D1)
so the crater depth is not confounded with R00 warming variance. Cheap, bounded, and it is the difference between "the
mode is calibrated" and "the mode passed once."

→ **Remediation:** `[new runs]`. Report min-across-seed PC BWT and max healthy |BWT|; if the min still fires the ✅ is
earned as stated, if not the mode returns to 🟡 (as P0.3.2 did to P0.3.0/P0.3.1).

**C2 — The genuine (deep/permanent) MAE pole is uncalibrated — the mode's defining open risk.** Every MAE fire is
shallow (−0.062/−0.063) and ~100% recoverable; the savings guard was structurally un-runnable on all of them
(P0.3.7/P0.3.8, savings fraction degenerate at 1.0). So it has **never** been shown that the label-free readers fire on
— let alone distinguish — a *deep, permanent* MAE crater, which is exactly the operationally dangerous case the
correlated long-horizon regime threatens. The only permanence evidence in the whole cluster is the P0.3.5 matched-LR
knee, and that is on the **supervised** fire, not the target. This is the forgetting analogue of the collapse audit's C1
(no incipient-severity calibration): the thermometer has been dipped in shallow-recoverable water but never in a genuine
deep crater.

→ **Remediation:** `[defer → Phase-1]` **is acceptable only if flagged as the mode's defining open risk**, not folded
silently into the ✅. P0.3.8 already showed a single hard shift will not reach it in-regime; the honest hand-off is
"deep-MAE-crater permanence is uncalibrated → Phase-1 (correlation × horizon)," stated as such.

**C3 — The reader set presupposes retained task-A data, which a *transient* stream denies.** Every forgetting reader
here queries task A at phase-B measurement time: the replay *control* (needs task-A data), BWT/FM (need a labelled
task-A probe), and the recon gap (needs held-out task-A images to reconstruct). The project premise is *transient* data
streams (seen ~once) + label-free. So in deployment there may be no replay arm to expose the recon FP (B2/P0.3.8), no
dense labelled per-era probe (A1), and no old-data recon reference. The calibration is valid *given* task-A access; the
readers may simply be unavailable without a design decision to retain a fixed labelled+unlabelled "canary" set.

→ **Remediation:** `[decision]` + `[defer → Phase-1]`. Record whether the Phase-1 monitor maintains a canary
set/old-data reference; if not, the calibrated readers do not deploy and Phase-1 needs a genuinely single-arm,
data-free-of-the-past forgetting signal (none is calibrated here). This is the forgetting counterpart of the collapse
audit's object-of-study mismatch (P0.2 C2).

**C4 — Cross-mode backbone coherence (shared with the collapse audit's C3).** Collapse was calibrated on SimSiam/Barlow
at the projector; forgetting on MAE via the recon reader. These are **mutually-exclusive** backbones — MAE has no
joint-embedding projector, SimSiam/Barlow no reconstruction reader — so at most one mode's calibration ports to Phase-1
as-is.

→ **Remediation:** `[decision]`. Record which SSL backbone(s) the streaming loop runs (cross-reference P0.2 audit C3);
scope which mode's instruments survive accordingly.

## D. Conclusions the data supports but the docs missed

**D1 — The freeze-encoder phase-A variant is the highest-value next lever, not a footnote.** P0.3.6 mentions freezing
the encoder during phase A (warm the decoder alone, preserving the raw R00 ≈ 0.89) only as a parenthetical "possible
follow-up." The dose-response data argue it is the concrete next move: crater depth is bounded by R00 (A3), the
joint-warm transient *depresses* R00 from ~0.89 to 0.65–0.71, and every MAE fire is shallow for want of well depth (C2).
Preserving R00 ≈ 0.89 restores ~0.18 of well the current fires throw away — plausibly the cheapest route toward a deep
crater (C2) *and* it removes the R00-variance confound (A3) in one change.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + `[new substudy]`-lite. Promote the freeze-encoder variant from footnote to the named
next experiment for reaching the genuine pole; naturally combines with C1's seed replication.

**D2 — The rigor is inverted: the supervised sanity-check is a *more complete* calibration than the actual target.** The
supervised fire (P0.3.4/P0.3.5) is N = 5 (4/5 fire, 5/5 hold), has CKA transferring cleanly 5/5, kNN corroborating
across 5 seeds, and a mapped matched-LR permanence knee. The target MAE fire (P0.3.6–P0.3.8) is seed-0, single-LR,
CKA-fails, savings-degenerate, permanence-untested. The docs correctly note the supervised fire "isn't the target" — but
under-draw the corollary: the *target* fire has not met the methodological bar the throwaway sanity-check set. Stating
this plainly is the cleanest justification for C1.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Add the one-line contrast; it reframes the whole cluster's status honestly.

**D3 — The from-scratch MAE floor (~0.38–0.40 on 10-way task A) is a reusable Phase-1 anchor.** P0.3.7/P0.3.8 both show
a random-init ViT-B linear-probes ~0.38 and a from-scratch MAE plateaus ~0.40 on task A — so all MAE task-A accuracies
live on `[0.38, 0.70]`, not `[0.10, 0.70]`. This compression is *why* the savings paradigm has no room (P0.3.7), and it
gives Phase-1 a concrete "near-total loss" reference: any MAE crater approaching ~0.40 is catastrophic, whereas the
current fires (bottom 0.645) are nowhere near it.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Record the floor as a named quantity; it sharpens what "deep crater" would even mean in
the MAE regime (C2).

Bottom line: the forgetting ✅ is earned for the narrow claim that **a two-sided MAE forgetting fire exists and the
labelled BWT/FM readers catch it**. As a *label-free, Phase-1-ready* calibration it is thinner than the ✅ implies: the
fire is single-seed / single-LR / shallow (unreplicated by the campaign's own standard, A2/C1), the lone surviving
label-free reader is a replay-exposure differential with an output-shift false positive (A1/A4), and the deep/permanent
pole the correlated regime actually threatens is entirely uncalibrated (C2) — while the two genuinely rigorous
calibrations (P0.3.4/P0.3.5) are on the wrong objective (D2).
