# Audit — P1.0.x open-loop harness engineering (2026-09-13)

Peer-review audit of the Phase-1 harness sub-studies — **P1.0.0** (loop-integration harness), **P1.0.1** (arm-alignment
divergence certificate), **P1.0.2** (deployment-prototype de-risker) — read against the substudy docs
([`phase1/P1.0.0.md`](../phase1/P1.0.0.md), [`.1`](../phase1/P1.0.1.md), [`.2`](../phase1/P1.0.2.md)), their
`latest-results` distillates, the [phase index](../phase1/index.md), and the promoted artifacts under
[`../phase1/artifacts/`](../phase1/artifacts/).

P1.0 is an **engineering** study — it builds the open loop and doesn't interpret signals — so this audit prioritizes
**code correctness** over scientific claims. The few science-shaped assertions it does make (the alignment certificate,
the trust map's Phase-0 provenance, the "sanity of readings") are reviewed peer-review-style. The numbers were
spot-checked against the artifacts and reproduce: the P1.0.0 C×I RankMe trajectories (7.27 → 2.36 etc.) and the
identical PC fire (loss floor −0.993, projector RankMe ×0.245); the P1.0.1 decomposition (data 0.0, matched 0.0, desync
worst 0.045 / 0.848 / 11.844 / 5.687); and the P1.0.2 BDD corpora (1385 rows = 5 seeds × 277 checkpoints, 17 delivered
legs, drift rising off zero and canary above chance on every seed).

Each point below has the shape **Issue** (what the audit found) / **Remediation** (what to do, work-type tagged) /
**Conclusion** TBD until remediations are addressed.

## Verdict

**P1.0 achieves what it set out to: a working, configurable, portable open loop, and the code behind it is
trustworthy.** The claimed numbers reproduce from the artifacts, the library is cleanly separated from Hydra and well
unit-tested, the `StreamingLoop` is genuinely reused untouched, and the P1.0.1 certificate is the standout — it earns
its "divergence is exactly zero" by *also* proving the measurement can see a divergence when one exists (the in-arm
desync control), so the zero is a certification, not a blind spot. The trust map is a real asset: a provenance-pinned,
self-describing registry that fails safe (unknown signals default to `uncalibrated`, never silently `trusted`).

Two gaps keep this short of "operationally complete for the mode studies," and both concern the **labelled canary** —
the one channel P1.3 / P1.4 will read as ground truth. First, the canary's held-out probe set is reserved per *drive
seed* while the warm well is warmed once on a *single* seed's pool, so every warm drive except the well's own seed
starts from a model that has already seen (unsupervised) the images it is later probed on — a soft transductive leak
(A1). Second, the "Tier-B sanity of readings" that the docs sell as the acceptance bar (RankMe in range, drift
accumulates, canary above chance) is **not codified** — `validation.passed` encodes only the Tier-A wiring checks, so
Tier B was an eyeball, not a reproducible gate (B1). Neither biases P1.0's own no-degradation verdict, but both must
close before the canary is load-bearing.

Where it lands: a **solid ✅ for its engineering scope**, with a short punch-list (A1, B1, and the canary's statistical
power, C2) to work before the mode studies lean on the corpus.

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- **The numbers reproduce, exactly.** Every headline figure in all three substudy docs was re-read from the promoted
    JSON/CSV and matches — including the deliberately identical PC fire across cells and the P1.0.1 desync ladder.
- **P1.0.1 is a genuinely rigorous certification, not a formality.** Isolating the stream RNG from the global RNG (data
    half) and Phase-0's per-arm re-seed (trajectory half), then *proving sensitivity* with the desync control, is the
    right way to retroactively license every Phase-0 `zip`. The insight — that the per-arm re-seed is what buys the zero
    — is correct and load-bearing.
- **The seams are clean and testable.** `cafl4ds.harness` / `deploy` / `deploy_corpus` are Hydra-free pure logic with
    dense unit tests; the corpus transforms carry no torch. The `StreamingLoop` really is reused verbatim.
- **The corpus-first hand-off is well-designed.** Event-relative coordinates (`steps_since_transition`, `is_transition`)
    are the right axis for comparing stateful trajectories across seeds; the graceful `pyarrow` skip keeps an expensive
    Gaudi drive from losing its corpus at the final write; the leg map's per-era scene/object histograms make each block
    self-describing.
- **The trust map fails safe.** `trust_for` never raises and defaults unknowns to `uncalibrated`, so a newly-added
    instrument cannot masquerade as trusted — exactly the right default for a registry stamped into every artifact.

## A. Code correctness & robustness

- **A1 — the canary probe reservation leaks into the warm well across seeds.**

    - **Issue.** `RegimeStream` reserves its held-out canary probe set with its *own* seed
        (`RegimeStream(seed=drive   seed)`), so each drive seed reserves a **different** 150-image probe set. The warm
        well, though, is produced by a single `warm_well.py` run at `seed=0` (the config default; the how-to-run warms
        once per backbone, not per seed) and trains on seed-0's training pool. For every drive seed ≠ 0, that pool
        contains almost all of the drive's held-out probe-query images, so the model the canary probes at every checkpoint
        was already shaped (unsupervised) by the very images it is scored on. The primary hand-off (`bdd_val_je_warm`,
        seeds 0–4) is precisely the leaky case; cold drives (`well=null`) are clean.
    - **Remediation.** `[engineering]` Pin the held-out reservation to a **fixed** seed independent of the drive/ ensemble
        seed (or reserve it once and share it), and warm the well from that same fixed split, so the probe set is
        identical across the well and every drive and disjoint from all SSL training. *Latent protocol gap, not a current
        bias.* It does not touch P1.0.2's no-degradation verdict, and empirically the warm kNN means (0.38–0.41) do not
        exceed the cold ones (0.37–0.42), so no inflation is visible today. But the canary becomes ground truth in P1.3 /
        P1.4 (legs b + c partly read off it), and a soft leak on the *primary* backbone is exactly the kind of thing that
        quietly flatters a forgetting read later. Close it before the canary is trusted.

- **A2 — the gate-arm path re-decodes the BDD source 2–3× per drive.**

    - **Issue.** `BDD100KSource.load()` decodes eagerly with no caching, and `RegimeStream.__init__` calls `load()`. With
        `gate_arms=true`, `_run_gate_arms` builds a fresh stream for the B5 monitor's eval-sets
        (`instantiate(config.monitor, eval_sets=instantiate(config.stream).eval_sets)`) *and* a fresh stream for the PC
        arm — each a full 8753-image decode — on top of the live arm's. So a default BDD run (the config ships
        `gate_arms: true`) decodes the corpus three times.
    - **Remediation.** `[engineering]` Cache the decoded `AttributedImages` on the source (or thread one stream/source
        instance through the arms), and/or default `gate_arms=false` (see B4). *Efficiency footgun, not a correctness
        bug.* The promoted runs used `gate_arms=false`, so the hand-off corpora were unaffected — but a user taking the
        shipped default onto BDD pays 3× the decode and may hit memory pressure the "compute-portable" framing does not
        advertise.

- **A3 — the correctness-of-premise check: no result-changing bug, one cosmetic weakness.**

    - **Issue.** A pass over the load-bearing edge cases found them handled sensibly: `collapse_gate` guards a falsy
        `init` (`drop = final/init if init else 1.0`, so a zero/absent initial RankMe reads as "did not fire" rather than
        dividing by zero); the loop stops on the first non-finite step; `all_finite` skips bools before the `isfinite`
        check; `leak_report` reads the *tail* spread, not absolute growth, so a caching allocator's warm-up is not
        mistaken for a leak. The one weak spot is `directional_report`'s `rankme_responded`, which uses `!=` — *any*
        change at all counts as "responded", so the check is near-vacuous.
    - **Remediation.** `[wording]` None required for correctness; optionally tighten `rankme_responded` to a
        magnitude/direction threshold if it is ever promoted from a smoke signal to anything load-bearing. *Premise is
        sound.* The code behind the numbers is trustworthy; the `rankme_responded` weakness is harmless because it is a
        number-free smoke readout, never a gate.

## B. Over-claims & doc/code discrepancies

- **B1 — "Tier-B sanity of readings" is asserted as the acceptance bar but is not codified.**

    - **Issue.** Both [`P1.0.2.md`](../phase1/P1.0.2.md) and the distillate frame acceptance as a two-tier validation
        whose Tier B confirms "RankMe in a sane range, drift accumulates, the labelled canary probes above chance." But
        `build_deploy_report`'s `validation.passed` is
        `all([finite, channels_present, channel_complete, trust_complete   (, memory_flat)])` — every one a Tier-A
        *wiring* check. None of the Tier-B plausibility criteria is computed anywhere; even `directional_report` (which
        owns `drift_accumulated`) is not wired into the deploy path. Tier B was therefore a manual read of the corpus, not
        a reproducible verdict.
    - **Remediation.** `[engineering]` Encode loose Tier-B thresholds (RankMe within `[lo, hi]`, final drift > 0, mean
        canary > chance + margin) into the report so the "sanity pass" is reproducible, and — if it stays manual — soften
        the docs to say so. *Structural gap.* The de-risker's whole selling point is "anyone can run the validation";
        today a future drive with an exploding RankMe or a chance-level canary would still stamp `passed=true` as long as
        the wiring holds. This is the single most worth-fixing item for reproducibility.

- **B2 — the trust map is not tested "against the monitor's emitted menu."**

    - **Issue.** [`P1.0.2.md`](../phase1/P1.0.2.md) and the `health_trust.py` docstring say the registry is "unit-tested
        for completeness against the monitor's emitted menu." `test_health_trust.py` never imports the monitor; it checks
        a hand-maintained `LOAD_BEARING_KEYS` list. If the monitor began emitting a new *load-bearing* signal, nothing
        would flag it as unmapped — it would silently default to `uncalibrated`.
    - **Remediation.** `[engineering]` Either add a test that diffs `LOAD_BEARING_KEYS` (or `TRUST_MAP`) against the
        monitor's actual emitted keys for each family, or reword the claim to "completeness against a curated load-bearing
        key list." *Over-statement, low blast radius.* The `uncalibrated` fallback makes a miss self-describing rather
        than dangerous, but the doc promises a stronger contract than the test provides.

- **B3 — "18-regime" vs "17-leg" is stated inconsistently across the docs.**

    - **Issue.** The index and both distillates describe the walk as over "18 regimes"; [`P1.0.2.md`](../phase1/P1.0.2.md)
        says "18 of them" in one place and "17-leg shift-walk / ~277 checkpoints over the 17-leg" in another. The
        artifacts resolve it: BDD's val split has **18** regimes, but one — **era 11, `dawn/dusk·foggy`** — has no
        training images left after the canary reservation, so `segments.json` streams only **17** legs.
    - **Remediation.** `[wording]` Adopt two canonical numbers with distinct roles: **"18 regimes"** whenever describing
        the *dataset* (BDD val's regime count), **"17 legs"** whenever describing the *walk actually streamed*. Fix the
        conflation where index/distillate say "18-regime walk" (a walk has legs, not regimes) to "17-leg walk over BDD's
        18 regimes", and add a one-line caveat in the detail doc: the rare `dawn/dusk·foggy` regime contributes no
        training batches after the held-out reservation, so 18 regimes deliver 17 legs. *Cosmetic but genuinely
        confusing.* Neither number is wrong; they answer different questions, and the docs currently use them as if
        interchangeable. The fix is a naming discipline, not a re-run.

- **B4 — the shipped `gate_arms` default disagrees with the documented hand-off default.**

    - **Issue.** `deploy.yaml` ships `gate_arms: true`, but [`P1.0.2.md`](../phase1/P1.0.2.md) says "the default hand-off
        case is `gate_arms=false`." So the bare Tier-A example command runs *with* PC/B5 co-logged (and, on BDD, pays the
        A2 triple decode); the promoted corpora only avoided this by passing `gate_arms=false` explicitly.
    - **Remediation.** `[decision]` Either flip the config default to `false` to match the hand-off (which also dodges
        A2), or reword the doc so "default hand-off case" plainly means "the setting we chose for the promoted corpora,"
        not the config default. *Minor, but a real footgun.* A user taking the shipped default onto BDD gets a heavier,
        slower run than the docs imply; aligning the two removes the surprise.

- **B5 — P1.0.1's certificate is earned on `EraStream`; P1.0.2 leans on it for `RegimeStream`.**

    - **Issue.** P1.0.2 justifies running one backbone per invocation with "the same config + seed reconstructs the same
        data regime by P1.0.1's certificate." But P1.0.1 measured that certificate only on the Phase-0 `EraStream`/STL
        path; `RegimeStream` is a different class.
    - **Remediation.** `[wording]` Note in the doc that P1.0.2 relies on the *data* half of the certificate — which is
        separately unit-tested for `RegimeStream` (`test_is_deterministic_in_seed`, same private-generator isolation) —
        and does not need the full in-arm-RNG re-lock decomposition re-run, since no arms are compared step-for-step on
        the deploy path. *A scope-transfer to make explicit, not a hole.* The claim P1.0.2 actually depends on — the diet
        is identical across backbones at fixed seed — genuinely holds and is tested; only the phrasing borrows a
        certificate proven on a sibling stream.

## C. Structural & test-coverage gaps

- **C1 — the Hydra entry-point scripts are unit-untested.**

    - **Issue.** Coverage is strong on the library modules (`harness`, `deploy`, `deploy_corpus`, `regime`, `warmup`,
        `health_trust`, `attributes`), but the glue in `run_deploy_harness.py` (the family-mismatch warning, the seed
        loop, the gate-arm wiring, the manifest assembly), `warm_well.py`, `measure_divergence.py`, and `run_harness.py`
        is exercised only by the actual runs. The promoted artifacts prove the runs work *once*; a regression in the glue
        (e.g. a broken seed loop or a mis-stamped manifest) would go uncaught.
    - **Remediation.** `[engineering]` Add a light end-to-end test per script on the `attr_synthetic` / `synthetic`
        source, asserting the corpus / comparison / divergence artifact lands with the expected shape — the Tier-A path is
        already fast and network-free, so this is cheap. *A real coverage gap, low current risk.* The runs are known-good
        today; the exposure is to *future* edits to the entry points, which nothing currently guards.

- **C2 — the canary barely clears chance, which under-powers the one *forward-looking* claim the corpus makes.**

    - **Issue.** This is *not* about interpreting a reading — P1.0.2 rightly interprets nothing, and "canary above chance"
        is a perfectly good *wiring* sanity check (it confirms the probe is fitted and learning something), which the data
        passes. The concern is narrower and forward-looking. The corpus doc advertises a specific downstream affordance:
        the paired (label-free, canary) columns "make *predict the canary from the label-free signals* a well-posed
        multivariate time-series problem this corpus provisions the data for." That affordance is only as good as the
        canary's signal-to-noise. With 3 scenes (chance 0.33) and 25 query images each (75 total), the kNN means sit at
        0.36–0.42 and individual checkpoints dip *below* chance (min 0.28 on warm MAE) — a target that moves ~6 points on
        a 75-image probe. A regression of label-free signals onto a target that noisy may have almost nothing to fit, so
        the advertised problem could be unanswerable *with this corpus*, even though the harness that produced it is
        sound.
    - **Remediation.** `[analysis / flag → P1.1]` Before anyone treats the canary as a downstream target, quantify its
        probe-power (a larger query set, more scenes, or per-checkpoint confidence intervals) and report whether the
        label-free → canary mapping is learnable above noise. Note too that the sanity diet is near-stationary; the mode
        studies drive *real* degradation, which should move the canary further — so this may self-resolve there, but that
        should be checked, not assumed. *A caveat on one sentence, not a defect in the harness.* It does not touch
        P1.0.2's engineering verdict or its no-degradation stance. It is worth flagging only because the corpus's *stated*
        value proposition leans on the canary carrying signal, and at this power that lean is thin. Softening the
        downstream sentence to a conditional ("a candidate downstream problem, pending a probe-power check") would make
        the claim honest today.

## D. Project context — does P1.0 earn the right to P1.1+?

The question the study must answer for the project is: *do we now have a trustworthy, configurable, portable, and
understandable harness for the open-loop experiments P1.1+ will run?* Mostly yes, with the punch-list above.

- **Trustworthy** — the arithmetic reproduces and the alignment method is certified, so a downstream reader can believe
    a co-logged trajectory. The one reservation is the canary channel (A1, C2), which the mode studies specifically
    depend on.
- **Configurable** — backbone, diet correlation (`block_size`), warm/cold, gate arms, logged-signal set, resolution, and
    device are all config-driven; the diet ships as machinery plus a default, with adequacy correctly deferred.
- **Portable** — the compute-agnostic core with hardware confined to the Docker/launcher edges is real, and Tier B ran
    on a Gaudi2 HPU from the same code CPU/CI runs. The A2 decode multiplier is the one portability wrinkle.
- **Understandable** — the corpus is genuinely mineable without the code (self-describing trust + leg map + manifest),
    and the docs are unusually candid about scope (STL-10 as smoke-only, the diet as "a default, not a certified
    pathology-inducer").

**The load-bearing gap before the mode studies** is not the STL→BDD substrate jump — that is de-risked for *wiring*, and
the diet's scientific adequacy is honestly owned by P1.1 / P1.3 / P1.4, not a P1.0 defect. It is the **canary becoming a
trustworthy ground-truth channel**: close the A1 leak, codify the B1 sanity bar, and establish C2's probe power. Those
three are what stand between "the harness runs on BDD" and "a BDD reading can be believed as evidence of (b)+(c)."

## E. Disposition

To be updated after all remediations are done and conclusions are in.

P1.0 is accepted as an engineering deliverable: the open loop is built, certified, and demonstrated end-to-end on real
BDD100K, and the code is sound with no result-changing bug found. The ✅ is earned for its stated scope (a running,
number-free rig), and the docs are candid about what they defer.

Before P1.1+ reads scientific signal through this harness, work the punch-list in priority order:

1. **A1** `[engineering]` — pin the canary held-out reservation to a fixed seed shared by the well and every drive
    (removes the soft leak on the primary hand-off).
1. **B1** `[engineering]` — codify the Tier-B sanity thresholds so "readings are plausible" is a reproducible verdict,
    not an eyeball.
1. **C2** `[analysis]` — quantify canary probe-power and whether the label-free → canary mapping clears noise.
1. **A2 / B4** `[engineering / decision]` — cache the BDD decode and reconcile the `gate_arms` default.
1. **C1** `[engineering]` and **B2 / B3 / B5** `[wording]` — script-level tests and the doc reconciliations.

Items 1–3 are the ones that gate trust in the corpus; 4–5 are hardening and consistency. None re-opens the ✅; together
they turn "the harness runs" into "the harness's readings can be believed."
