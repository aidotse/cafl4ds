# Audit — P0.2.x (collapse-mode instrument calibration)

Peer-review-style audit of the collapse failure-mode sub-studies (P0.2.0 → P0.2.1 → P0.2.2 → P0.2.3 → P0.2.4), conducted
2026-08-10 against the substudy docs, their `latest-results` distillates, and the promoted artifacts under
[`../phase0/artifacts/`](../phase0/artifacts/) (`P0.2.2/`, `P0.2.3/`, `P0.2.4/`). Numbers were spot-checked against the
JSON envelopes and reproduce; this is a review of *conclusions*, not arithmetic.

Each point carries a **→ Remediation** line tagging how it should be addressed: `[wording]` (doc edit, no runs),
`[analysis]` (post-hoc on existing data, no new runs), `[engineering]` (build + confirm), `[new substudy]`, `[decision]`
(needs an architectural call), or `[defer → Phase-1]`.

## Verdict

As a calibration exercise this is strong, unusually honest work — genuinely two-sided, run against *two* collapse
sub-modes (point + redundancy), theory-first with confirm/refute, and willing to turn the gate on itself (P0.2.4). The ✅
on the collapse mode is defensible **for the claim it actually makes**: the instruments were characterized two-sided
against a maximally-forced collapse vehicle, at both surfaces, with a known false-positive map.

The ✅ over-reads toward **operational readiness for Phase-1**. Stripped down, the deployment story is thinner than the
summary implies, and there is one structural gap the sub-study never touches (C1). Grouped below as over-claims (A),
tensions/contradictions (B), structural gaps (C), and conclusions the data supports but the docs did not draw (D).

## Strengths (so the criticism is calibrated)

- Two-sided, per-*sub-mode* calibration (point via SimSiam, redundancy via Barlow) — most work calibrates one collapse
    flavour and generalises; this does not.
- The **surface-as-calibration-axis** finding (backbone vs projector) is real, non-obvious, and mechanistically
    explained by the terminal `BatchNorm(affine=False)`.
- Honest nulls (`offdiag_cov` under point collapse), honest refutations, and E8's estimator-variance analysis that
    demotes its *own* noisiest instrument (`mean_feature_var`).
- An explicit "Phase-1 carry-overs, NOT closed here" section in P0.2.2 — good scientific hygiene.

## A. Over-claims / conclusions stronger than the evidence

**A1 — In the *actual* Phase-1 corner, the "calibrated suite" collapses to one marginal detector.** The target regime is
correlated × single-pass. In P0.2.2 E1 that corner has **exactly one firing instrument: RankMe @ proj at 2.17×** — right
on the 2.0 bar. `variance@proj` (1.01), `uniformity@proj` (0.28), and everything at the backbone are quiet there. E4
shows that single-pass projector fire is **LR-conditional** (fires only at ≥ base LR; E8 flags its straddle at 0.5×). So
the honest operational bottom line for Phase-1 is: *one* instrument, at ~2×, LR-conditional, with a documented eval-set
FP mode (A2). That is far more precarious than "✅ Suite calibrated two-sided" conveys. The multivariate suite is a
long-horizon/IID luxury; it does not survive to the deployment corner.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]`. The `index.md` status cell has been rewritten to state this
plainly (single marginal detector in the target corner). Carry the single-pass projector detector as an explicit Phase-1
risk; hardening it (more seeds, a fallback beyond one instrument) is Phase-1 work, not re-calibration. A small
`[new substudy]` (P0.2.5) to shore up the single-pass projector fire at higher seed count is optional and low-priority
relative to C1.

**A2 — "RankMe @ proj is not fooled by low-diversity data" drops a load-bearing conditional.** As *run*, RankMe@proj
false-fired on low-diversity data on 3/4 seeds. Option B correctly attributes this to the eval-set cap (few-class
probe), not the model — but the "fix," matched-class era-evaluation, is **asserted, never built or demonstrated**.
Phase-1 era eval sets *are* few-class, so operationally the instrument *is* fooled until that eval machinery exists.

→ **Remediation:** `[engineering]` + `[wording]`. Build matched-class era-evaluation and re-run the low-diversity
Option-A cell to confirm the FP vanishes (a small, bounded task — best done now since it is the concrete Phase-1
exposure). Until then, downgrade the distillate from "not fooled" to "not fooled *once matched-class eval is in place*
(unbuilt)".

**A3 — "The instruments complement each other" is weaker than it sounds, and E7 half-admits it.** E7 shows the projector
trio is ρ ≈ 0.85–0.93 correlated — they carry largely the same magnitude signal, differing only in *where* they cross
threshold. For point collapse, RankMe@proj strictly dominates variance@proj and uniformity@proj (fires earlier, less
noisy, more corners). The only instruments adding genuinely distinct capability are `offdiag_cov@proj` (redundancy
sub-mode) and `variance@proj` (the point-vs-redundancy discriminator).

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Reframe the P0.2.2 conclusion to the sharper, supported statement: the operational set
reduces to **{RankMe@proj, offdiag_cov@proj, variance@proj}**; uniformity@proj and the entire backbone surface are
near-redundant. No new runs.

**A4 — Magnitudes are cited as headline numbers while admitted to be thread-count/device-dependent.** P0.2.2 E1 states
the separation magnitudes "shift with thread count / device" and only the binary verdict is stable — yet distillates
lead with "fires ~13–20×," "6–11×," etc.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Add a consistent one-line caveat wherever magnitudes appear that they are illustrative
and non-portable; prefer verdict language in the distillates. No new runs.

## B. Contradictions / internal tensions

**B1 — The alignment/uniformity *pair* was never operationalized, yet uniformity is used standalone.** P0.2.2's theory
table states uniformity "is only meaningful read *jointly* with alignment" and alignment "alone is not a collapse
detector." But the calibration then reports **`uniformity@proj` as a standalone FIRE** and never produces a joint
align–uniformity verdict. So either uniformity-standalone is fine (contradicting the stated theory) or the pair still
needs a joint gate that does not exist.

→ **Remediation:** `[analysis]` + `[wording]`. Alignment was wired and logged, so the E1 runs likely already contain
what a joint verdict needs — produce it post-hoc (no new runs) *or* reconcile the doc by dropping the "uniformity needs
alignment" theory claim, since the data shows uniformity@proj fires standalone. Prefer the reconciliation; the joint
verdict is only worth computing if uniformity is kept in the operational set (per A3 it may not be).

**B2 — Prototype entropy was listed as a *calibrated* instrument but was declared out of scope. `[resolved]`** The
`index.md` Collapse row's *"Instruments calibrated"* column listed "prototype entropy," but P0.2.2 declares it out of
scope with no substudy, so the cell overstated coverage. Prototype-assignment entropy is the canonical collapse detector
for *prototype/clustering-based* SSL (SwAV, DINO): it reads how uniformly a batch spreads across the learnable
prototypes, and collapse shows as the batch-averaged assignment piling onto one prototype (entropy → 0). It is a valid
metric — **but only where prototypes exist.** This project's collapse vehicles are SimSiam (joint-embedding) and Barlow
Twins (decorrelation); neither has a prototype head, so there is no assignment distribution to take the entropy of, and
the metric is not implemented anywhere in `cafl4ds/measurements.py`.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` — **done (2026-08-10):** dropped from the `index.md` instruments cell (and from the
status-cell clause), since it is not a valid consideration for the joint-embedding collapse vehicles used here. It would
return only if Phase-1 adopts a prototype SSL method (DINO/SwAV), which would then need its own collapse ablation and a
dedicated calibration substudy.

**B3 — The two-sided standard is only partially met single-pass.** P0.2.1's central finding is that a single-pass
healthy backbone *cannot be independently certified healthy* (it is mid-transient). The projector "rescue" reports
PC/healthy *separation* in that corner — but a two-sided calibration requires the quiet side to be a *certified-healthy*
reference, which single-pass does not provide at either surface. So the single-pass projector fire is a *relative*
separation between two under-developed arms, not a fire against a certified-quiet baseline; the docs treat these as
equivalent.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + `[defer → Phase-1]`. State explicitly that the single-pass projector fire is a relative
separation, not a certified-quiet-baseline two-sided pass. A genuine single-pass healthy certification is likely
unattainable at toy scale (that is P0.2.1's own finding), so this is a wording fix plus a Phase-1 carry, not a re-run.

## C. Structural gaps to close before Phase 1

**C1 — [Highest] Sensitivity to *partial/incipient* collapse is entirely uncalibrated.** Both vehicles drive the model
to a mathematically-forced *global* trivial optimum — the "boiling water" end. There is no controlled *partial*-collapse
continuum confirming the instruments read intermediate severity monotonically. This is the one thing Phase-1 monitoring
actually needs — **early** warning of gradual degradation — and it is the only failure mode without a severity
dose-response (contrast P0.4's dose-response + lead-time, and P0.3's erosion-depth operating-point map). The thermometer
has been dipped in ice and boiling water but never checked for a scale in between.

→ **Remediation:** `[new substudy]` (recommended P0.2.5), partially mitigated by D3. Add a partial-collapse
dose-response: interpolate the anti-collapse strength (e.g. a soft stop-gradient / predictor-scale knob, or blend the
collapse and intact losses) and confirm the projector detectors' readings vary *monotonically* with collapse depth on
the *healthy-to-collapse* continuum — not just on the deep-forcing side D3 already covers. If a new toy substudy is
judged too costly pre-Phase-1, `[defer → Phase-1]` is acceptable **only if** flagged as the mode's defining open risk;
it should not be silently folded into the ✅.

**C2 — The object-of-study mismatch: we trust the surface we do not care about.** The project studies *backbone*
representation health (P0.2.2 itself calls the backbone "the downstream-relevant surface"), yet the calibration
concludes we must read the *projector* and treat the backbone as FP-prone/insensitive. Under *forced* collapse the two
surfaces are coupled (the ablation collapses everything); whether projector geometry faithfully tracks backbone health
under **gradual, non-forced** degradation — the Phase-1 case — is never tested.

→ **Remediation:** `[new substudy]`, fold into C1. On the partial-collapse continuum, track whether projector geometry
*predicts backbone probe-accuracy degradation* (ties to C4). One combined "gradual-degradation" substudy answers C1, C2,
and C4 together and is the highest-value pre-Phase-1 experiment.

**C3 — Cross-phase coherence: the collapse suite is joint-embedding- and terminal-BN-specific, but P0.3/P0.4 use MAE.**
The entire projector-surface conclusion rests on SimSiam/Barlow's terminal `BatchNorm(affine=False)` projector — MAE has
no such joint-embedding projector or point-collapse mode. Which backbone does the Phase-1 streaming loop actually run?
If MAE, this suite may be moot (or needs an MAE-collapse analogue); if a joint-embedding method, the *projector rule*
ports but none of the toy-scale thresholds do.

→ **Remediation:** `[decision]` (needs your architectural call), then `[wording]`. Resolve and record — in
`project-plan/index.md` or the phase-0 index — which SSL backbone(s) the streaming loop uses, and scope the collapse
suite's applicability accordingly. No experiment; a clarification that prevents discovering this in Phase-1.

**C4 — "Healthy" is defined circularly by the instruments; no independent downstream anchor is surfaced.** The healthy
baseline is certified because RankMe re-expands (the U-shape) — the instrument certifies itself. The monitor *has*
kNN/linear probes and the canonical corner ran with probes on, but the probe-accuracy contrast (healthy vs collapsed) is
never cited as independent ground truth that "healthy" is *actually useful*.

→ **Remediation:** `[analysis]` or `[engineering]`-lite. Surface the probe-accuracy contrast for the certified corner.
The promoted artifacts dropped the probe series, so if it is not recoverable from a Hydra run dir, one confirmatory
re-run of the canonical corner (probes on) recovers it; add a sentence to P0.2.1 grounding "healthy" in downstream
utility. Low cost, high credibility payoff; naturally combines with the C1/C2 substudy.

## D. Conclusions the data supports but the docs missed

**D1 — The projector detectors are weakest at *intermediate* correlation, not full — a "mild-correlation valley."** The
docs read E3 as "projector detectors are correlation-*invariant*." The per-seed data says something sharper. Robust
(min-across-seed) separations:

|  | IID | bs128 | bs256 | class_blocked |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| RankMe@proj | 11.28 | **6.14** | 7.26 | 8.34 |
| var@proj | 13.91 | 2.70 | **1.81** (below bar) | 2.65 |

Both projector detectors bottom out at *mild/intermediate* block sizes (RankMe@proj min at bs128; var@proj min at bs256,
where it *fails* the bar), then partially recover at full class-blocking. The co-occurrence across two independent
instruments argues structural, not seed noise. It is the **opposite** of "invariant," and it matters: Phase-1 streams
are *partially* correlated (partial-shuffle) — precisely this valley, not the IID or full-block extremes the calibration
is most confident about.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]` + optional `[new substudy]`-lite. Correct "correlation-invariant" to "fires across all
correlation levels but is weakest at intermediate block sizes," and add a Phase-1 caveat. Optionally confirm
structural-vs-noise with a few extra seeds at bs128/bs256 (cheap targeted run).

**D2 — Backbone per-dim variance under point collapse is *sign-unstable*, not merely null.** The docs report
`var@backbone` at IID×40ep as "null (min 0.50)." The per-seed reality is stronger: the collapse arm's backbone variance
is 15× *lower* than healthy on seed0, ~2× *higher* on seed3 (sep 0.50) — the separation *changes sign* across seeds. So
the backbone variance reading is not just insensitive; it is not even directionally consistent about which arm collapsed
— a cleaner justification for "read at the projector" than "it is quiet."

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Upgrade the P0.2.2 E1 finding from "null" to "sign-unstable across seeds." No new runs.

**D3 — A collapse-*depth* dose-response already exists in the horizon data — partially answering C1.** E2's PC limb
sinks monotonically as the forcing horizon grows (RankMe@bb PC: 2.69→2.48→2.22→2.07→1.99→1.93→1.92→1.77→**1.67** over
ep5→80) while healthy saturates — so the separation is monotone in collapse *depth*. The docs read this only as "40 is a
healthy-signal plateau," never drawing the dual conclusion: **RankMe's reading is monotone in collapse depth on the
deep-collapse side**, real (if partial) evidence the instrument scales with severity. It does *not* close C1 — that gap
is the *incipient/healthy-side* continuum this deep-forcing sweep never populates.

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Draw the positive conclusion in P0.2.2 E2 / the P0.2.1 horizon section and cross-link it
as partial mitigation of C1. No new runs.

**D4 — The two horizon sweeps (P0.2.1 exploratory vs P0.2.2-E2) do not reproduce numerically, confirming "only verdicts
port."** P0.2.1 reports healthy RankMe@bb saturating ~5.5→5.79 (ep40→80); the E2 re-run gives 5.46→**6.13** — still
climbing at ep80, a +12% rise, not the "flat through 80 plateau" P0.2.1 claims. The verdict-level story (monotone ratio,
robust ≥2× from ~25–40 ep) is identical, but the magnitudes and the "saturates by 40" sub-claim are not reproducible
across the two sweeps. This is a *positive* meta-finding: it independently validates the docs' own "report the verdict,
not the number" caveat, and mildly qualifies "40 is a plateau."

→ **Remediation:** `[wording]`. Note that the two sweeps agree at verdict level but not magnitude (reinforcing the
device/thread caveat), and soften "saturates by 40 / plateau" to a *verdict-level* plateau. No new runs.
