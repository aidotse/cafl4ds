## P1.1.2

From [the label-free lens sub-study](phase1/P1.1.2.md) — the third P1.1 piece. Phase 0 left the MAE backbone with no
label-free way to tell that its representation is getting worse. P1.1.1 then damaged the real MAE model on purpose, at
graded doses, so a candidate signal can be tested against damage of known size. P1.1.2 runs that test on every
candidate: new per-layer lenses (similarity to the starting model, rank and intrinsic dimension, read after each of the
encoder's 12 blocks) and every signal the monitor already logs.

**The approach.** A signal must fire on the damage the labelled canary catches. It must also stay quiet on one special
control: re-initializing the top 4 blocks, which changes the weights a lot and costs the canary nothing. A signal that
fires there is detecting "the weights were scrambled", not lost information. That is the trap Phase 0 fell into with its
best earlier candidate (P0.5.3). All the reads come from checkpoints saved earlier, so no new training was needed.

**What it found.**

- **One signal survives: CKA drift.** It measures how far the representation's content has moved from the starting
    model, and the monitor already logs it. It fires on the damage the canary catches, rises with the size of the
    damage, stays quiet on the re-initialization control, and sometimes fires on damage the canary misses.
- **It is a change detector, not a quality reader.** It cannot say whether a representation is good, only that it has
    left a starting point we certified as good. That assumption — a competent, certified start — is one Phase 0 never
    made, and it keeps Phase 0's finding intact: no internal MAE signal reads quality on its own.
- **Every other signal fails, including Phase 0's.** Phase 0's MAE candidates are re-tested here on the real backbone:
    rank, uniformity, the variance/covariance terms, loss and alignment. They fail again, now for a named reason. Rank
    does not track the damage. Uniformity, the variance/covariance terms and alignment fire on the control: they read
    the weights, not the information. Loss tracks damage only against a matched control run. Three new candidates fail
    the same way: Procrustes distance, cosine drift and intrinsic dimension, the literature's favoured label-free proxy.
- **The per-layer lenses localize but add no detection power.** MAE damage shows in every block. On the joint-embedding
    backbone, the correlated diet's benign compression lives in the last third of the blocks and the projector.
- **CKA drift is not yet a quiet monitor.** Healthy adaptation also moves the model away from its start. So CKA drift
    fires on most healthy correlated drives and on every optimizer restart, by a margin only about half the damage's.
    And in Phase 0, on the same model, a natural forgetting event barely moved it.

**Status: ✅ complete.** The screen leaves one candidate. Whether CKA drift can tell information loss from benign
adaptation is [P1.1.3](phase1/P1.1.3.md)'s question; certification on a natural MAE failure is P1.3's. Meanwhile, P1.3
can already use it to compare an arm against a matched control.
