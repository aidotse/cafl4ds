## P1.1.2

From [the label-free lens sub-study](phase1/P1.1.2.md) — the third P1.1 piece. Phase 0 left the MAE backbone with no
label-free way to tell that its representation is getting worse. P1.1.1 then damaged the real MAE model on purpose, at
graded doses, so a candidate reader can be tested against damage of known size. P1.1.2 runs that test on every
candidate: new per-layer lenses (similarity to the starting model, rank and intrinsic dimension, read after each of the
encoder's 12 blocks) and every signal the monitor already logs.

**The approach.** A reader must fire on the damage the labelled canary catches. It must also stay quiet on one special
control: re-initializing the top 4 blocks, which changes the weights a lot and costs the canary nothing. A reader that
fires there is detecting "the weights were scrambled", not lost information. That is the trap Phase 0 fell into with its
best earlier candidate (P0.5.3). All the reads come from checkpoints saved earlier, so no new training was needed.

**What it found.**

- **One MAE reader survives: CKA drift.** It measures how far the representation's content has moved from the starting
    model, and the monitor already logs it. It fires on the damage the canary catches, rises with the size of the
    damage, stays quiet on the re-initialization control, and sometimes fires on damage the canary misses.
- **Five other readers read the weights, not the information.** Procrustes distance, intrinsic dimension, cosine drift,
    uniformity and the variance/covariance terms all fire on the control. Intrinsic dimension, the literature's favoured
    label-free proxy, is among them.
- **The per-layer lenses localize but add no detection power.** MAE damage shows in every block. On the joint-embedding
    backbone, the correlated diet's benign compression lives in the last third of the blocks and the projector.
- **CKA drift is not yet a quiet monitor on its own.** It stays quiet on fresh healthy drives in shuffled order. But it
    also fires on most healthy correlated drives and on every optimizer restart, by a margin only about half the
    damage's.

**Status: 🟡 runs complete (Stage 1).** The screen is done. A screen cannot certify a reader, so certification waits for
a natural MAE failure (P1.3). How to handle benign correlated-drive movement — the planned Stage 2 — awaits a decision;
see the [detail doc's handover](phase1/P1.1.2.md#remaining-work--handover).
