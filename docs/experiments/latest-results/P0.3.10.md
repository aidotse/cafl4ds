## P0.3.10

From [the far-domain MAE forgetting test at a deep well](phase0/P0.3.10.md). A late P0.3 addendum, opened after the P0.6
JE arc, to close a reviewer-facing gap in the "MAE resists forgetting" conclusion: the whole P0.3 arc tested only
**near-domain** phase-B shifts (CIFAR-100, phase-scramble, grayscale), never a genuinely **far** domain — so resistance
was never tested where it is most likely to break.

**Result: MAE resists a far shift at depth (the resistance is earned, not headroom-limited).**

The test is made fair in two ways. First, a competent, *stable* task-A well: a plastic MAE encoder keeps settling under
continued reconstruction, so R00 is read at its reconstruction-competence plateau (reached via a decoder-first warm plus
a short consolidation), where the A-only control genuinely holds flat and any crater is real. Second, the far phase B is
run at two domains that *bracket* task-A reconstruction difficulty — DTD textures (harder to inpaint, the conservative
primary, applying more update pressure than continued task-A training) and EuroSAT satellite imagery (easier, the
bracket) — because in MAE the reconstruction-loss magnitude sets the update pressure, so a trivially-easy far domain
would resist for the wrong reason.

Across both far domains, N = 5, the two-sided (PC − healthy) crater is *shallow and never fires*, while the control
holds every seed. The forgetting is real (every seed leans that way, drift mostly agrees) but far below the bar — so a
competent MAE *substantially resists* even a fair, harder-pressure far shift at depth. A clean side-finding: the
*easier* domain craters *more* than the harder one, so the (small) forgetting magnitude tracks the phase-B domain's
gradient *direction* — how much it overwrites task-A features — not its inpainting difficulty. The result *reinforces*
the P0.3 study verdict (MAE largely resists; the genuine deep/permanent pole stays the real-scale Phase-1 question) and
*discharges the P0.6 audit's C2*: on the identical far EuroSAT shift a joint-embedding vehicle forgets where MAE
resists, now with both read against controls that hold — so the "MAE resists the far shift" leg of the JE pivot is
established, not provisional. Artifacts:
