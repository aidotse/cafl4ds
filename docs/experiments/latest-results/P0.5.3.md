# P0.5.3 — quality-vs-lever test for `alignment_strong` (decoder dose-down)

From the [decoder-strength lever analysis](phase0/P0.5.3.md), The decisive follow-up to [P0.5.2](P0.5.2.md)'s one
candidate MAE-native reader, and the audit's C1.

**Status: runs complete (seed-0 dose-down)**. Verdict — `alignment_strong` is a confounder: it reads the
over-powered-decoder *regime*, not representation quality, so it is refuted as a quality reader.

**The test.** A decoder dose-*down* at the P0.5.2 operating point (embed 96, from-scratch, seed 0): weaken the shortcut
arm's decoder from the firing 512×8 toward the healthy 64×2, and trace `alignment_strong` against the kNN crater.

**The result.** The reader fires *flat across the whole decoder axis* — including at 128×4 and 256×6, where the shortcut
arm has *no quality deficit at all* (its probe matches or beats healthy — and stays *silent* on the genuine +0.042
crater the *mask* lever produces. So the fire is coupled to decoder strength (RankMe frac tracks it, 1.17 → 1.04) and
decoupled from quality. Fire-at-*negative*-crater is a cleaner disproof than the planned matched-depth comparison.

**What it means.** This confirms the audit's lean and closes the P0.5 mode: `alignment_strong` is not the MAE-native
quality reader P0.5 set out to find. The mode's ✅ re-anchors on its two robust negatives (the P0.2 geometry suite is a
trap on MAE; MAE resists the shortcut → the degradation is shallow). More broadly it settles the MAE half of one
structural finding — *no internal MAE signal reads representation quality label-free* (the loss decouples, the geometry
readers mis-signal, invariance is untrained/lever-confounded), the same wall the forgetting arc ([P0.3.9](P0.3.9.md))
hit. Label-free quality monitoring on an MAE backbone needs a labelled canary, or a joint-embedding projector (the
[P0.6.0](P0.6.0.md) test). Detail + the dose-down table → [phase0/P0.5.3](../phase0/P0.5.3.md).
