## P0.3.9

From the [seed-stability + well-depth de-confound substudy](phase0/P0.3.9.md) — the [P0.3 audit](audits/P0.3.md)'s
robustness check of the [P0.3.6](phase0/P0.3.6.md) label-free MAE fire, the single result that had flipped the
forgetting mode to ✅.

**Result: the P0.3.6 fire does not replicate, but a phase-B-dwell lever at a corrected deep well delivers a seed-stable
5/5 two-sided fire — so the forgetting instruments are calibrated (recommend 🟡 → ✅), while the genuine/permanent pole
stays a Phase-1 risk.**

- **Seed replication (C1) refutes the promoted fire.** At the P0.3.6 operating point only **1 of 5 seeds fires** (worst
    PC BWT −0.010 vs the −0.05 bar). Seed 0 reproduces the promoted −0.062, so it is a **lucky-seed draw, not a code
    fault**. The healthy/quiet arm is robust across all five seeds — it is the fire limb that fails.
- **The as-found freeze-encoder D1 was warming-confounded — retracted.** Freezing the encoder through *all* of phase A
    holds R00 ≈ 0.89 and gives a 5/5 PC crater but 0/5 gates, because the encoder-only checkpoint pairs with a fresh
    decoder and freezing defers that decoder's **warm-up transient** into phase B, cratering the *healthy* control too.
    The **corrected decoder-first D1** pays the transient inside phase A, settling a deep *trained-in* R00 ≈ 0.84 whose
    control **holds 5/5** — and a matched-`epochs_a` control shows **well-depth is a genuine partial lever** (deep fires
    3/5 vs shallow 1/5).
- **The last inch is phase-B pressure, not well-depth.** The replay-preventable differential (~0.05) is depth-invariant,
    so more well cannot close 3/5 → 5/5. Doubling the phase-B dwell at the fixed deep well (`epochs_b` 20 → 40) fires
    **5/5** (worst PC BWT −0.065, mean −0.074; healthy holds 5/5, max |BWT| 0.028). The extra epochs are *asymmetric* —
    replay refreshes task A per-epoch — so the PC crater deepens while the control holds. So the two-sided BWT/FM
    instruments now have a **seed-stable positive control**, resolving C1's downgrade cause.
- **But seed-stable ≠ permanent.** A savings check on the deepest crater (−0.085) bottoms task-A at 0.742, far above the
    from-scratch floor ~0.40 — ~fully recoverable (savings ≈ 1.0). Doubling the toy horizon buys **severity, not
    permanence** (P0.3.5). MAE still resists *catastrophic* forgetting; the genuine deep/**permanent** (savings ≈ 0)
    pole needs the **correlation / real-scale** regime, not more toy dwell.

So the forgetting instruments are two-sided seed-stable-calibrated at a deep-well + `epochs_b=40` operating point
(recommend restoring the mode to ✅ for that), while the genuine/permanent MAE pole (C2) remains the standing **Phase-1**
open risk — now bounded by the finding that even a *doubled* toy horizon does not reach it, against the from-scratch
floor (~0.40) as the "near-total loss" reference. Artifacts:
