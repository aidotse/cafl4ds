## P0.3.9

From the [seed-stability + well-depth de-confound substudy](phase0/P0.3.9.md) — the [P0.3 audit](audits/P0.3.md)'s
robustness check of the [P0.3.6](phase0/P0.3.6.md) label-free MAE fire, the single result that had flipped the
forgetting mode to ✅.

**Result: the fire does not replicate, and preserving the deep well does not rescue it — the forgetting mode returns to
🟡.**

- **Seed replication (C1) refutes the fire.** At the P0.3.6 operating point only **1 of 5 seeds fires** (worst PC BWT
    −0.010 vs the −0.05 bar). Seed 0 reproduces the promoted −0.062, so it is a **lucky-seed draw, not a code fault**;
    by the campaign's own P0.3.2 min-across-N standard the ✅ is not earned. The healthy/quiet arm is robust across all
    five seeds — it is the fire limb that fails.
- **The freeze-encoder de-confound (D1) preserves the deep well but breaks the two-sided gate.** Freezing the encoder
    through phase A holds R00 ≈ 0.89 (vs the joint-warm 0.65–0.71) and deepens the PC crater to a **seed-stable 5/5**,
    yet **0/5 gates pass**: the encoder trains cold in phase B on both arms, so the replay control craters too and the
    replay-preventable gap stays ≈ 0.04 — no larger than joint-warm.
- **The load-bearing insight: R00 ≈ 0.89 is a frozen-feature transfer readout, not trained-in competence.** Any encoder
    training — even replaying task A — erodes it common-mode, so a deeper well buys no deeper *forgetting*. Well-depth
    was never the bottleneck; MAE's replay-preventable forgetting is intrinsically shallow at this horizon.

The genuine deep/permanent MAE pole is not reachable by a cheap toy lever — it is a real-scale correlation × horizon
question (**Phase-1**). Artifacts: [`artifacts/P0.3.9/{c1_seedsweep,d1_freeze}/`](phase0/artifacts/P0.3.9/).
