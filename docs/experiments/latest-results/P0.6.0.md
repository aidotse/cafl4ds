## P0.6.0

From [the joint-embedding forgetting vehicle & the projector-surface label-free reader](phase0/P0.6.0.md). Motivated by
the forgetting arc's awkward truth ([P0.3.9](phase0/P0.3.9.md)): an MAE backbone largely *resists* forgetting, and on a
label-free stream there is no trustworthy, past-data-free forgetting reader (BWT/FM need labels; `cka_drift` did not
transfer; the recon gap is weak and confounded — the audit's C3/A1). This substudy tests a **candidate answer to that
gap**: a joint-embedding (JE) SSL vehicle (SimSiam / Barlow, already in-repo) instead of MAE.

**Status: designed, awaiting runs — harness extension to build; nothing run yet.**

The bet is two-fold: a JE method may forget more readily than MAE (no reconstruction anchor to resist with), and — the
real deliverable — its **projector** surface, where P0.2 calibrated the collapse suite as a label-free read, may furnish
a **single-arm, past-data-free** forgetting reader the MAE backbone denies (the C3 canary gap). The vehicle is the
proven P0.3.6/P0.3.9 split-task design (task A = Imagenette, cross-distribution phase B, matched-replay healthy control)
with the *only* new variable the backbone; init is pretrained-on-A (degradation-from-competent). As in P0.5, the ground
truth is the frozen-probe BWT and the deliverable is an instrument verdict — does any label-free projector reader
(`alignment_proj` / `uniformity_proj` / drift) track the crater two-sided? A fire answers C3 on the JE backbone; a clean
null is also a result. The built-in trap: SimSiam's native failure is collapse, so `rankme_proj` must stay full-rank on
the PC arm (the collapse-specificity precondition) for a fired reader to mean *forgetting*, not collapse. Scoped as a
**single** substudy — permanence/savings, from-scratch-vs-pretrained, and any new reader are Phase-1, not new Phase-0
substudies.
