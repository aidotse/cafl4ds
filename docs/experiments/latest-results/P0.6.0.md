## P0.6.0

From [the joint-embedding forgetting vehicle & the label-free projector *canary* reader](../phase0/P0.6.0.md). Targets
the forgetting arc's **A1** gap ([P0.3.9](../phase0/P0.3.9.md)): MAE resists forgetting and leaves no *trustworthy*
label-free reader on an SSL stream (its recon gap is weak and confounded). This substudy — the anchor of the P0.6 pair —
tests a **joint-embedding (SimSiam)** vehicle and asks whether its projector surface furnishes that reader.

**Status: complete — a *qualified* A1 success; the label-free reader is canary *drift*, not the projector *geometry* the
hypothesis named.**

Three findings. **(1) The JE vehicle forgets where MAE resists — but the shift has to be far.** A near-domain phase B
does not clear the fire bar (a near-miss, the two arms nearly level); only a genuinely *far* domain (e.g. satellite
imagery) craters it. Even then the crater is **shallow and seed-fragile** — it fires on most seeds, not all, and adding
phase-B pressure *moves* the lone miss rather than removing it (the recurring Phase-0 signature). The healthy control is
A-only (train phase B on task A alone); matched replay proved an unstable far-shift control and is recorded as a
secondary ablation. **(2) The deliverable reader is representation drift, not the projector geometry the hypothesis
named.** The *primary* A1 hypothesis — that the P0.2 projector-geometry suite would read forgetting — does not hold.
`uniformity` mis-signals (it even flips direction on some seeds); calibrated to read *collapse*, it does not port, since
collapse ≠ forgetting. Its sibling `alignment` separates the arms on every seed here, but it never tracks how much was
forgotten, and a re-run of the same setup ([P0.6.1](P0.6.1.md)) separates on only two seeds of five, so neither geometry
reader is carried. What carries the mode is **drift on a task-A canary**, which tracks the crater two-sided on every
seed in both runs, and links to crater depth at the deeper well. `rankme_proj` stays full-rank throughout, certifying
the crater is forgetting, not collapse. **(3) A vehicle contrast *suggests* the objective is the cause.** Swapping the
SSL objective to MAE, on the same far-shift vehicle, MAE appears to *resist* where SimSiam churns — masked
reconstruction anchors the representation, joint-embedding does not. The P0.6.0 contrast only *suggested* this (the MAE
arm's well-depth and init were confounded with the objective), but the clean deep-well far-domain MAE test
([P0.3.10](../phase0/P0.3.10.md)) has since **settled it**: a competent, stable MAE resists both far domains (0/10)
where the JE vehicle fires 4/5, so the "MAE resists the far shift" leg is established, not provisional.

One caveat carried forward: the drift reader stays **canary-bound** (needs retained task-A data), so its
*past-data-free* form is [P0.6.1](../phase0/P0.6.1.md). Permanence and magnitude are Phase-1.
