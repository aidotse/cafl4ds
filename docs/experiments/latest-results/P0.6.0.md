## P0.6.0

From [the joint-embedding forgetting vehicle & the label-free projector *canary* reader](phase0/P0.6.0.md). Targets the
forgetting arc's **A1** gap ([P0.3.9](phase0/P0.3.9.md)): MAE resists forgetting and leaves no *trustworthy* label-free
reader on an SSL stream (its recon gap is weak and confounded). This substudy — the anchor of the P0.6 pair — tests a
**joint-embedding (SimSiam)** vehicle and asks whether its projector surface furnishes that reader.

**Status: complete — a *qualified* A1 success; the label-free reader is canary *drift*, not the projector *geometry* the
hypothesis named.**

Three findings. **(1) The JE vehicle forgets where MAE resists — but the shift has to be far.** A near-domain phase B
leaves SimSiam unmoved (both arms land at the same probe); only a genuinely *far* domain (e.g. satellite imagery)
craters it. Even then the crater is **shallow and seed-fragile** — it fires on most seeds, not all, and adding phase-B
pressure *moves* the lone miss rather than removing it (the recurring Phase-0 signature). The healthy control is A-only
(train phase B on task A alone); matched replay proved an unstable far-shift control and is recorded as a secondary
ablation. **(2) The deliverable reader is representation drift, not the projector geometry.** The *primary* A1
hypothesis — that the P0.2 projector-geometry suite (uniformity/alignment) would read forgetting — is **refuted**: those
readers are calibrated to read *collapse*, and pointed at a forgetting crater they mis-signal (uniformity even flips
direction). Collapse ≠ forgetting, so a collapse reader does not port; **drift on a task-A canary does**, tracking the
crater two-sided on every seed. `rankme_proj` stays full-rank throughout, certifying the crater is forgetting, not
collapse. **(3) A vehicle contrast isolates the objective.** Swapping only the SSL objective to MAE, on the identical
far-shift vehicle, MAE *resists* where SimSiam churns — masked reconstruction anchors the representation,
joint-embedding does not.

Two caveats carried forward: the far-domain MAE arm ran at a shallow (phase-A-*eroded*) well, so a rigorous deep-well
far-domain MAE test is [P0.3.10](phase0/P0.3.10.md); and the drift reader stays **canary-bound** (needs retained task-A
data), so its *past-data-free* form is [P0.6.1](phase0/P0.6.1.md). Permanence and magnitude are Phase-1.
