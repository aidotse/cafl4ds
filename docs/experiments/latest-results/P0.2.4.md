## P0.2.4

From the [false-positive stress sub-study](phase0/P0.2.4.md) — the quiet-side calibration promoted from P0.2.2's flagged
E6. No PC arm: two healthy SimSiam arms (a canonical reference + an atypical-healthy test) are paired via the existing
envelope, and the verdict is **inverted** — a run passes iff **no** instrument false-fires. Three atypical-but-healthy
stressors (low-diversity data, heavy augmentation, deliberate undertraining), 4 seeds each, 40-epoch IID STL-10.

**Status: complete.** The study did its job — it **found concrete false-positive boundaries**. The suite is *not*
false-positive-free at this toy scale; every stressor trips at least one detector (the acceptance criterion's "document
the boundary" branch).

- **`offdiag_cov` @ backbone is the dominant FP liability** — false-fires robustly under undertraining (5–39×, all 4
    seeds) and low-diversity data (3.1×), because immature/low-diversity backbones have genuinely correlated features.
    **`offdiag_cov` @ projector stays quiet in all three stressors.** With [P0.2.3](phase0/P0.2.3.md) (fires for the
    right reason at the projector), this closes a clean rule: **read off-diagonal covariance at the projector only.**
- **RankMe** survives undertraining and heavy aug but false-fires on low-diversity data (2.1× bb, 2.4× proj) — its
    predicted natural risk, though partly an eval-set-cap artifact (2-class probe) rather than a pure model false-fire.
- **Heavy augmentation** is the most robust regime (3/4 seeds fully quiet); the lone graze is `mean_feature_var` @
    backbone on the worst seed, re-confirming variance is the noisiest instrument and never a standalone gate (P0.2.2
    E8).
- **The projector surface is again the trustworthy core** — every robust false fire is at the backbone or in the
    eval-set-confounded low-diversity regime; a third vindication (after P0.2.2, P0.2.3) of reading the suite at the
    projector.
