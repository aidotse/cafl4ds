## P0.2.4

From the [false-positive stress sub-study](phase0/P0.2.4.md). The gap from P0.2.3: everything E1–E4 varies is how the PC
and healthy arms *separate*; none stresses the quiet side with a *healthy-but-atypical* representation, so the suite's
false-positive rate is untested — the failure mode that most hurts the loop (a false fire mis-aims selection). P0.2.4
pushes a genuinely-healthy arm into regimes that superficially resemble collapse (low-diversity data, heavy
augmentation, deliberate undertraining) and asks whether any instrument false-fires. Result: The study **finds concrete
false-positive boundaries**, and **every projector-surface false fire is an eval-set artifact**.

We test two setups:

1. (A) Two healthy SimSiam arms (a canonical reference + an atypical-healthy test) are paired via the existing envelope.
    The verdict is **inverted** — a run passes iff **no** instrument false-fires.

    The suite is *not* false-positive-free at this toy scale; every stressor trips at least one detector.

    - **`offdiag_cov` @ backbone is the dominant FP liability** — false-fires robustly under undertraining (5–39×, all 4
        seeds) and low-diversity data (3.1×), because immature/low-diversity backbones have genuinely correlated
        features. **`offdiag_cov` @ projector stays quiet in all three stressors.** With [P0.2.3](phase0/P0.2.3.md)
        (fires for the right reason at the projector), this closes a clean rule: **read off-diagonal covariance at the
        projector only.**
    - **RankMe** survives undertraining and heavy aug but false-fires on low-diversity data (2.1× bb, 2.4× proj) — its
        predicted natural risk, though partly an eval-set-cap artifact (2-class probe) rather than a pure model
        false-fire.
    - **Heavy augmentation** is the most robust regime (3/4 seeds fully quiet); the lone graze is `mean_feature_var` @
        backbone on the worst seed, re-confirming variance is the noisiest instrument and never a standalone gate (P0.2.2
        E8).
    - **The projector surface is again the trustworthy core** — every robust false fire is at the backbone or in the
        eval-set-confounded low-diversity regime; a third vindication (after P0.2.2, P0.2.3) of reading the suite at the
        projector.

1. (B). Compare the atypical-healthy arm with a forced-collapse arm in the *same* stressed regime, so the eval-set cap
    cancels — resolving every fire from the first setup into artifact vs. genuine blind spot. Result: within the
    2-class regime the matched collapse pole still separates from the atypical arm on all 4 seeds, so the instruments
    discriminate collapse in-regime. **Every backbone false fire is genuine** (RankMe @ backbone low-diversity is a
    real blind spot; `offdiag_cov` @ backbone undertraining is genuine saturation, same eval set). This upgrades "read
    the suite at the projector only" from empirical pattern to mechanistically confirmed, and closes the low-diversity
    caveat: RankMe @ proj is *not* fooled by low-diversity data.
