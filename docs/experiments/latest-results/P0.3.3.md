## P0.3.3

From the [batch-size probe substudy](phase0/P0.3.3.md). [P0.3.2](P0.3.2.md) showed toy STL-10 does not forget robustly
because task B under-learns. P0.3.3 tests the one untried mechanism lever — a smaller training batch (more B-only steps
\+ gradient noise, near-full-batch → noisier) — as a last attempt to induce forgetting at toy scale before escalating the
dataset.

**Result: batch size does not rescue it — no batch produces a robust fire, and R11 stays flat.**

- **0/5 fire at every batch size.** `batch_size=64` is uniformly positive (no forgetting); `batch_size=32` nudges the
    median just negative with a lone best seed still short of −0.05 and within seed noise.
- **R11 ≈ 0.55–0.59 across all three batch sizes** — task B never learns appreciably above its floor regardless of
    batch, so the missing respecialisation pressure never appears. `cka_drift` is correspondingly tied (PC ≈ healthy).
- **The bottleneck is upstream, not optimisation.** R11's invariance to batch size rules out step-count/gradient-noise
    as the cause and confirms P0.3.2's diagnosis: MAE's reconstruction objective on toy STL-10 simply does not build a
    task-B-discriminative representation strong enough to erode task A.

**Toy STL-10 is exhausted as a forgetting vehicle** Calibration escalates to CIFAR-100 ([P0.3.4](P0.3.4.md)). Nothing
promoted — ephemeral negative runs.
