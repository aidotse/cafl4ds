## P1.1.0

From the [healthy-movement null sub-study](phase1/P1.1.0.md) — the first P1.1 piece. Phase 0 warned that a healthy
backbone *also* moves its health instruments, so a reading off the correlated BDD drive means nothing until we know how
much a healthy drive moves. P1.1.0 measures that null with a **matched IID control** — the walk's exact images, steps
and batch sizes, only shuffled — so any walk-vs-IID gap comes from the correlation alone. It also sizes the labelled
canary's statistical power (the P1.0 audit's C2), and sweeps how **familiar** the drive's frames are to the warm-start
well, because P1.3 will drive on new frames and needs to know whether familiarity narrows the null.

**The harness additions.** The matched IID order; a larger probe set stratified by time of day, so the canary reads day
/ dusk / night separately (the channel a forgetting read needs); drift anchored to the frozen well; grad norm in the
corpus; full-method checkpoints at every leg boundary; and a well/drive split with a familiarity knob that leaves the
drive bit-identical at every level. Walk-vs-IID gaps are tested with an exact seed-permutation test over all checkpoints
at once. An independent code review verified the mechanics and corrected the statistics before analysis.

**What the deep-well null shows (the primary result).** The wells are a pretrained ViT-B warmed on the well pool until
its labelled competence stops moving. That stop matters: continued self-supervised training first pulls a pretrained
model's competence *down* before it levels off, and a drive started mid-slide would inherit the slide as fake
degradation. From such a settled well, the healthy IID drive barely moves its instruments. Most of the "benign movement"
the shallow null showed was the well still settling. On the joint-embedding backbone, the correlated walk still
compresses the projector and now also pulls the representation measurably further from the well, at both familiarity
levels — while a canary with real headroom stays flat. The compression is smaller than Phase 0's firing bar, so whether
it is harmful collapse or benign compression stays P1.1.1's first question. MAE leaves almost no trace of the
correlation at all: walk and IID are nearly indistinguishable, and the forgetting signature is absent. Familiarity still
shows no effect. The canary resolves gaps of a few points and now sits well clear of both chance and the supervised
ceiling, so "no loss" is a real reading rather than a floor effect.

**What the shallow-well null added.** The earlier sweep, on barely-trained tiny-ViT wells, stays as the five-level
familiarity read. It found the same JE projector direction and no familiarity effect. Its larger null movement and its
MAE drift separation did not survive at depth.

**How the deep wells were reached.** A competence study showed the from-scratch tiny ViT was the binding limit — it
cannot learn the canary even with labels — while the frames carry the signal. That moved the deep wells to a pretrained
ViT-B. A scout fixed the learning rates and a two-sided competence stop, read on a larger labelled selection set kept
apart from the measurement probes.

**Status: ✅ complete.** The deep-well null, the familiarity check and canary power are in; the full numbers and caveats
are in the [detail doc](phase1/P1.1.0.md). Two caveats carry forward: the last checkpoint of each drive is a 3-image
batch (its loss and grad norm are noise), and the drive restarts the optimizer (a short, matched transient). The
overlap-0 leg checkpoints for P1.1.1's recovery tests are parked temporarily inside the BDD data directory and must be
moved once dedicated storage lands. Artifacts are under [`phase1/artifacts/P1.1.0/`](phase1/artifacts/P1.1.0/) (deep run
in `deep/`).
