## P1.1.4

From [the reading foundation for P1.3's setup](../phase1/P1.1.4.md) — the fifth P1.1 piece, opened by the
[P1.1 audit](../audits/P1.1.md). P1.1 earned the right to read degradation on a short drive, from MAE-started wells,
with a recipe P1.2 later replaced. P1.3 now runs elsewhere: longer passes, three different starting models including a
DINO one, a repaired MAE recipe, and forgetting read on the joint-embedding backbones rather than on MAE. P1.1's method
carries over to that setup; its calibrated numbers do not.

**The approach.** Rebuild the reading tools on P1.3's own three arms, with P1.1's method unchanged. A fourth is
calibrated beside them: DINO continued with its own objective, which P1.2 set aside on its linear read but which
fine-tunes best of all. It may yet join P1.3, and its objective's drift toward collapse needs its own watch. Four parts,
each held to a bar fixed in advance:

- the healthy null and the single-drive bands, per arm, first on existing drives and then on fresh healthy seeds. The
    labelled canary is split in two, following P1.2's lesson: the linear read judges information, and the
    nearest-neighbour read counts as damage only when a fine-tuning read drops beside it;
- damage of known size on the two joint-embedding arms and the DINO one, and a search for a label-free reader that sees
    it there;
- the same on the repaired MAE recipe, plus a natural MAE failure (the old recipe's) to test the middle-block reader
    P1.1.3 left;
- how small a loss on a past driving regime the labelled referee can resolve, and how large a held-out pool that needs.

**Why it matters.** P1.3 reads degradation on an arm only once that arm's readers pass these bars. Without them, a
healthy run could read as damaged and a damaged one as healthy.
