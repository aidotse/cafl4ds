# P0.4.0

From the [divergence substudy](phase0/P0.4.0.md) Phase-0 instability/divergence mode is calibrated — the gradient-norm
instrument fires two-sided, with lead time, and is specific to divergence.

**Result:** two-sided gate **PASS**. Healthy stays finite and learns, grad norm bounded; the PC goes non-finite (top arm
at step 4), and **every diverging arm clears the healthy peak by ≥100×** — the portable separation verdict. (The single
`~10⁶×` contrast the gate prints is a truncation-sensitive illustration, not a portable number: it is a min over
firing-arm peaks, so it swings ~35× across seeds/batches of the same phenomenon while the `≥100×` verdict holds.) Clean
dose-response with a stability threshold, speeding up monotonically.

- **The payoff — lead time.** At the just-past-threshold operating point the grad-norm blow-up crosses at **step 3**
    while the loss does not fail until **step 8** → **5 steps of label-free lead** before the loss NaN. Lead time is
    most likely not generalizable, but we show that it can exist.

- **Specificity + robustness.** P0.2's forced-collapse SimSiam PC keeps grad norm bounded → grad norm does not fire on
    collapse. The fire reproduces at `batch_size=16` (same threshold, same 5-step lead) → not a batch-size artifact.

- **Key finding — vehicle = MAE + SGD (not AdamW).** Divergence is an *optimization* runaway (reproduces at toy scale,
    unlike forgetting). It needs an unbounded loss (MAE MSE; SimSiam's bounded cosine collapses instead) and an undamped
    optimizer: SGD's step `∝ ‖g‖` lets the gradient feed back → a *gradual* runaway with lead time; AdamW normalizes the
    step, damping the loop → a knife-edge with **no lead time**. So the grad-norm early-warning signal is trustworthy
    under SGD-like dynamics and largely mute (as a *leading* indicator) under an adaptive optimizer — an
    instrument-scope caveat, not a universal property. **Both poles are now preserved** (`adamw_seed0`: the grad norm
    blows to `~10⁵–10⁶` yet the normalized step absorbs it, a one-step NaN only at `lr=100`, lead `None`). Since
    Phase-1's optimizer/loss is deliberately open, this is a **design recommendation**, not just a caveat: to keep the
    grad-norm early-warning value, favour an undamped optimizer + raw MSE; under AdamW + `norm_pix` the grad norm
    degrades to a coincident NaN alarm and early-warning becomes a Phase-1 open problem.
