# P0.4.0 — LR-driven divergence, the gradient-norm gate ✅ DONE

**Status: DONE (2026-08-06).** The 3rd and last Phase-0 mode (instability/divergence) is calibrated — the gradient-norm
instrument fires two-sided, with lead time, and is specific to divergence.

**Result (seed 0, reproduced seed 1; STL-10 tiny MAE img32, SGD momentum 0.9, warmup & clip off):** two-sided gate
**PASS**. Healthy (`lr=1`) stays finite and learns (loss `1.34→0.996`), grad norm bounded (steady `~0.026`, peak
`0.44`); PC (`lr=30`) goes **non-finite at step 4**, grad norm to `4×10¹²` — a **1.2×10⁶×** contrast. Clean
**dose-response** with a stability threshold at `lr=3.0` (NaN@8), speeding up monotonically (`lr=10`→NaN@5,
`lr=30`→NaN@4).

**The payoff — lead time.** At the just-past-threshold operating point (`lr=3`) the grad-norm blow-up crosses at **step
3** while the loss does not fail until **step 8** → **5 steps of label-free lead** before the loss NaN.

**Specificity + robustness.** P0.2's forced-collapse SimSiam PC (loss→`−0.99`) keeps grad norm **bounded** (peak `2.3`,
no non-finite) → grad norm does not fire on collapse. The fire reproduces at `batch_size=16` (same threshold, same
5-step lead) → not a batch-size artifact.

**Key finding — vehicle = MAE + SGD (not AdamW).** Divergence is an *optimization* runaway (reproduces at toy scale,
unlike forgetting). It needs an **unbounded loss** (MAE MSE; SimSiam's bounded cosine collapses instead) **and an
undamped optimizer**: SGD's step `∝ ‖g‖` lets the gradient feed back → a *gradual* runaway with lead time; **AdamW
normalizes the step, damping the loop → a knife-edge** (instant-NaN at `lr=100`, or grad `10³–10⁶` absorbed at
`lr=1–10`) with **no lead time**. So the grad-norm early-warning signal is trustworthy under SGD-like dynamics and
largely mute (as a *leading* indicator) under an adaptive optimizer — an instrument-scope caveat, not a universal
property.

**Build.** Instrumented the streaming loop (reused live in Phase 1): pre-clip grad-norm capture + non-finite guard +
divergence-stop in `loop.py` (`UpdateStats`), `grad_norm`/`finite` on each per-step loss record in `run_log.py`, and a
new `scripts/positive_control_instability.py` + `configs/positive_control_instability.yaml` (+ `configs/optim/sgd.yaml`)
running the healthy arm + PC LR sweep and evaluating the two-sided contrast + divergence + lead-time gate.
