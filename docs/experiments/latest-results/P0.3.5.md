## P0.3.5

From the [forgetting-fire audit substudy](phase0/P0.3.5.md). The P0.2.4 analogue — but an **audit of an existing fire**,
not a constructed quiet-side calibration: with a real fire in hand ([P0.3.4](P0.3.4.md)), does that fired BWT over-read
along either of two *orthogonal* axes? Two guards on the operating-point pretrained fire: a **kNN cross-probe** (genuine
vs apparent — is the degradation real or a linear-probe artifact) and a **recoverability** re-learn (permanent vs
recoverable — granting it's real, does it heal). The load-bearing framing: a genuine fire and a recoverable one are the
same fire on different axes.

**Result: the fire is genuine (not apparent) — and genuine forgetting is *recoverable*: severity ≠ permanence, cleanly
demonstrated at the calibrated operating point.**

- **Cross-probe — genuine, and the linear gate is conservative.** Re-reading the N=5 fire with kNN craters *deeper* than
    the gated linear probe, healthy holding under both — so both probes agree task A degrades (real, not
    linear-inaccessibility). The refit-linear BWT *under*-reports vs a frozen-metric probe.
- **Recoverability — genuine forgetting is recoverable, and the operating point is the proof.** Recovery re-learns
    **task A** at the LR matched to each arm's phase-B LR. The calibrated operating-point crater recovers **100% back to
    R00** in a true plateau — a genuine fire that fully heals, so severity ≠ permanence. Deeper craters (dug at higher
    LR) recover only *partially* within the fixed 256-step budget we imposed; this is not the load-bearing result.
- **Reading (two axes, kept separate).** A BWT fire certifies degradation *now* (genuine), not that it is permanent —
    different questions, different guards. The deployment takeaway: at the calibrated operating point a fired crater is
    genuine but fully recoverable, so treat a fire as "degradation now" and use a cheap replay / kNN check to tell
    transient from permanent before escalating.
- \*\*For the gated supervised BWT a calibration would be low-value \*\* — it is immune to the linear-inaccessibility
    (apparent) artifact **by construction** (it refits). But that immunity does *not* transfer to the **label-free MAE
    readers** cafl4ds deploys - their apparent quiet-side is **uncalibrated**. A dedicated, *isolated* constructed
    apparent-forgetting calibration for those readers (correlation as a candidate driver) is future work.

Promoted the kNN + recovery `comparison.json`s to [`artifacts/P0.3.5/`](phase0/artifacts/P0.3.5/). This does **not** by
itself flip the forgetting **mode to ✅**: P0.3.4 + P0.3.5 both run on a *supervised* fire, and the calibration cafl4ds
needs is instrument **+ MAE, label-free** ([P0.3.6](phase0/P0.3.6.md)) — so the mode stays 🟡 until then.
