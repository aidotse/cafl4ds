## P1.2.5

From [strong starts, the payoff curve and a wider panel](../phase1/P1.2.5.md) — the last P1.2 piece. Until here, the
evidence that a backbone gains by learning on the stream rested on one start: MAE's pretrained weights, continued with a
joint-embedding objective. That start is weak under the readout P1.2 relies on, which flatters any adaptation. P1.2.5
asks whether adapting the best available start still beats keeping it frozen, and after how much of the stream.

**The approach.** Two backbones start from DINO, the strongest frozen encoder tried: one continues with DINO's own
objective, the other with the joint-embedding objective P1.2 already used. The earlier MAE-started backbone is repeated
beside them. Each is compared with its own starting point, frozen, on the untouched evaluation frames, at checkpoints
along one pass. Five supporting reads: a wider panel of tasks; controls trained on non-driving images; a check that the
health instruments see DINO's known failure; an exploratory read of the gain by object size and by the stream's regimes
(time of day, weather); and both joint-embedding backbones run with and without the fine-tuning protections, with every
readout taken at every checkpoint.

**What it found.**

- **Adapting a strong start pays, but narrowly.** DINO continued with the joint-embedding objective beats frozen DINO on
    the two main tasks, by about four points on scene at the learning rate a three-seed search chose. Non-driving images
    do not reproduce the gain, so it comes from the driving data. But it shows on almost no other task, and the higher
    rate costs the nearest-neighbour read the selection filter uses. By the pre-registered table the claim is
    "Qualified". It is a close call: a test over the seeds alone would count gains on three of the four wider tasks.
    Those gains are smaller than the wider tasks' evaluation sets can resolve, so the gain is firm on the main tasks and
    its reach beyond them is open, not ruled out.
- **The fine-tuning protections guard the geometry, not the gain.** Run without them, both joint-embedding backbones
    gain as much on the main tasks' linear probe. What the protections keep is the nearest-neighbour read and how well
    the encoder still fine-tunes. A recipe chosen on the linear probe alone cannot see that trade.
- **DINO's own objective does not suit this data.** Driving frames look so alike that DINO's centring step flattens its
    own training target. Its continued training adds nothing a linear probe reads, though it helps the nearest-neighbour
    read and fine-tuning; it is not carried forward. It is the objective that fails, not the features: frozen DINO stays
    the best frozen start.
- **The MAE-started backbone passes again, with a smaller gain.** Repeated under slightly changed conditions, its linear
    gain holds but shrinks, and its nearest-neighbour gain repeats only mid-drive. With the protections, road-free
    scenes reproduce part of its gain; without them, its link to the driving data reads as strong as first found
    ([P1.2.4](P1.2.4.md)). Its gain is the broadest of the three, and it sits on small objects — pedestrians, trucks,
    buses smaller than one image patch.
- **Within one pass, the gain carries and holds.** Night frames gain before the stream reaches night, and daytime frames
    keep their gain after it leaves. What does erode is the nearest-neighbour read: in every drive with the
    joint-embedding objective it gains in the first half of the pass and gives that back in the second. How often an
    object appears in the stream does not decide whether it gains.
- **What it means.** One pass is too short to pick a backbone. P1.3 runs DINO's joint-embedding backbone, the
    MAE-started one and MAE as equal arms over a longer horizon, and chooses only after. Recipe tuning moves there too,
    choosing on more than one readout and more than one seed: quality on the linear probe and fine-tuning, with the
    nearest-neighbour read as a constraint and as a check on the selection filter's input. It reads every readout at
    every checkpoint, not only at the drive's end, on this wider panel fixed in advance, with enough evaluation images
    to settle the breadth question. The scope is still one pass over a fixed domain: the evaluation frames share the
    training frames' distribution.
