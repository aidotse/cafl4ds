## P1.2.1

From [the gate read](phase1/P1.2.1.md) — the second P1.2 piece, and the one that carries leg (a) of the Phase-1 exit
gate. The project assumes a backbone living on a stream should keep learning from it. P1.2.1 tests that directly: a
pretrained encoder trains on one full pass of driving frames it has never seen, and is compared with the same encoder
left frozen (**B5**).

**The approach.** Both backbones start from the identical off-the-shelf encoder, with their training heads settled
beforehand while the encoder stayed frozen. So at step 0 each *is* B5, and any difference at the end comes from
adaptation alone. Each backbone drives the correlated walk with 5 seeds, beside a shuffled control. The adapted encoders
are then scored on the untouched BDD val split, on two tasks (the scene, and whether a traffic light is present among
frames matched for scene and time of day) and at every label budget. The pass bars were fixed before any data was read.

**What it found.**

- **The joint-embedding backbone passes.** Its features carry more linearly decodable information than the frozen
    encoder's on both tasks, by margins well outside the noise. The gain builds over the first half of the drive and
    holds.
- **The pass is real but not uniform.** Under fine-tuning, the adapted encoder is a better start on the traffic-light
    task and a slightly worse one on the scene task. The nearest-neighbour read is flat overall.
- **MAE fails, cleanly.** It reads below the frozen encoder on every measure of both tasks. Most of the loss arrives in
    the first hundred steps, which points at the training recipe more than at the data.
- **Correlation reshapes the joint-embedding backbone's geometry, not its information.** The correlated walk costs its
    nearest-neighbour accuracy at night, against the shuffled control, while the linear gain holds. That matters for the
    project's selection filter, which works on embedding distances. It is also a concrete forgetting lead for P1.3. A
    caveat came later: with P1.2.4's gentler training recipe, the night-time loss disappears, so the lead depends on the
    recipe.
- **What it means.** Leg (a) passes, but on the secondary backbone only. P1.3's primary vehicle is MAE, so the next
    step, P1.2.2, diagnoses MAE's recipe before P1.3 starts. That diagnosis traced MAE's loss to a too-small decoder; a
    repaired MAE matches the frozen encoder without beating it (P1.2.2). JE's pass held up and grew under a better
    recipe (P1.2.4).
