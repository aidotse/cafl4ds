## P0.3.2

From the [multi-seed robustness substudy](phase0/P0.3.2.md) — the P0.2.2 analogue. P0.3.0/P0.3.1 read the forgetting
fire from a *single* seed, near the −0.05 gate threshold; P0.3.2 asks whether it survives the **weakest** replicate
(min-across-N) before it can be trusted.

**Result: the toy-scale fire is REFUTED — it was a single-seed probe-noise artifact. The instruments are sound; the
STL-10 vehicles/animals vehicle hosts no robust forgetting event.**

- **Not seed-stable.** N=5 at the P0.3.0 corner (60/60 probes): only **1/5** seeds fire (the weakest even has the *wrong
    sign*); the healthy quiet side also breaks 3/5. Both arms swing in *both* directions ⇒ probe-read noise (BWT is a
    difference of two 240-image reads).
- **Bigger probes kill it.** Re-run with 200/200 probes (drawn from the STL-10 test split to keep the training budget):
    the variance *collapses* and 0/5 fire, task A flat/up, no crater. This is a correctly-behaving estimator converging
    to ≈0 (no forgetting), not to the threshold.
- **Mechanistic smoking gun: R11 ≈ 0.56.** Task B (animals) barely learns above its ~0.53 four-way floor, so the
    representation never respecialises enough to overwrite task A — exactly P0.3.0's negative-result #2 (MAE learns
    transferable structure). `learnedA` is stable (~+0.09): the erodable delta exists, but there is no crater to erode
    it.
- **P0.3.0/P0.3.1 re-scoped, not discarded** — their durable value is instrument plumbing + the noise-floor
    characterisation (a 4-way forgetting BWT needs ~200/200-scale probes) + the drift demotion.
