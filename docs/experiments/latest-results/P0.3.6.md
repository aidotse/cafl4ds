## P0.3.6

From the [SSL-MAE label-free forgetting substudy](phase0/P0.3.6.md). The piece the forgetting mode was missing: a
**label-free, self-supervised (MAE)** catastrophic-forgetting fire — read by the signals available in deployment, not
just the supervised upper-limit of [P0.3.4](phase0/P0.3.4.md).

**Result: a pretrained MAE backbone continuing MAE training fires a clean two-sided crater at seed 0 — but the fire does
NOT replicate across seeds (audit), so the forgetting mode is 🟡, not ✅.** Matched-distribution replay is what makes the
seed-0 fire possible, and the MAE recon-loss monitor tracks it (CKA drift does not).

- **The unlock — cross-dataset + matched replay.** A same-dataset split showed *no* differential (replaying a CIFAR
    "task A" can't protect an ImageNet-sourced well). Fix: **task A = Imagenette** (pretraining-distribution proxy, MAE
    encoder probes R00 ≈ 0.89), **task B = CIFAR-100** (the shift), **replay = Imagenette** (matched to the competence
    source). Replay protects only when it matches where the competence came from.

- **The fire (seed 0, `lr=3e-4`).** After a short phase A warms the fresh decoder and pins R00 ≈ 0.71, phase B craters
    it: **PC BWT −0.062** (fires), **healthy replay BWT −0.003** (holds), **18.5× FM contrast** — but at a **single
    firing LR flanked by non-fires** (non-monotone: +0.012 → −0.020 → −0.062 → −0.048), with crater depth partly
    tracking R00 well-depth variance. Not a knife-edge, but not a clean dose-response either.

- **Label-free monitors — recon works, CKA doesn't.** The **MAE recon gap corroborates** (+0.036: PC's held-out
    Imagenette reconstruction degrades while replay's improves). **`cka_drift` does *not* transfer** from the supervised
    fire — tiny (~0.004) and not reliably ordered. The label-free reader reduces to the **MAE recon gap** — but it is a
    **replay-exposure differential** (a coarse presence/absence indicator that needs a two-sided control and over-fires
    ~10× on cosmetic output shifts, P0.3.8), not a graded forgetting tracker — a narrowing correction to the P0.3.4
    reading.

- **Levers confirmed.** The pretrained-deep-well lever (crater depth bounded by R00) carries from supervised (P0.3.4) to
    MAE; a fresh MAE decoder must be **warmed** (short phase A), not skipped, or phase-B drift is decoder-noise rather
    than task-differential forgetting.

- **Seed-stability — the fire does not replicate (→ [P0.3.9](phase0/P0.3.9.md)).** The dose-response is **seed 0**; the
    audit's follow-up substudy [P0.3.9](phase0/P0.3.9.md) shows it fires in only **1/5** seeds (a lucky-seed draw), and
    the freeze-encoder rescue that preserves the deep well can't fix the two-sided gate — so **the label-free MAE
    calibration is not seed-stable**.

The seed-0 fire is a real detection, but not seed-stable → **the forgetting mode is 🟡** (see
[P0.3.9](phase0/P0.3.9.md)). Whether correlation × long horizon breaks MAE's resistance is the defining **Phase-1**
question. Promoted the seed-0 LR dose-response `comparison.json`s to [`artifacts/P0.3.6/`](phase0/artifacts/P0.3.6/)
(the C1/D1 N = 5 sweeps live under [`artifacts/P0.3.9/`](phase0/artifacts/P0.3.9/)).
