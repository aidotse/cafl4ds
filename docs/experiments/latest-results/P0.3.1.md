## P0.3.1

From the [forgetting operating-point substudy](phase0/P0.3.1.md) — the follow-on to [P0.3.0](P0.3.0.md), set out to
strengthen the crater above the toy-scale noise floor and settle the drift verdict. The evidence redirected it: instead
of a deeper crater, P0.3.1 concludes the small crater is a *task/scale ceiling*, not an under-tuned regime.

**⚠ Revised by [P0.3.2](P0.3.2.md):** this substudy's "passing" reads were all single-seed (seed-0) probe-noise
artifacts — multi-seed + more precise probes show no seeds forget at this corner; the calibration gate itself does not
pass here. Real calibration escalates to CIFAR-100 ([P0.3.4](P0.3.4.md)).

**Result: the forgetting instruments (per-era probe / BWT / FM) are calibrated at toy scale; the crater cannot be pushed
deeper by scale or erosion knobs; drift is characterised-and-demoted for a precise reason.**

- **Forgetting is non-monotonic in capacity, peaking at P0.3.0's intermediate 256/8.** Scaling *up* refutes the original
    hypothesis — a bigger backbone learns task A *better* but buffers against forgetting; scaling *down* also forgets
    less. Tripling B-only erosion (300→900) leaves the crater flat — respecialization saturates. So P0.3.0's small
    crater sits at the interference sweet spot and is a genuine ceiling.
- **BWT/FM are two-sided calibrated across the whole sweep.** They fire on real forgetting and the healthy replay
    control holds in every run; the gate "failures" (scale↑, shrink) are **true negatives** (forgetting genuinely
    absent), not misses.
- **Drift verdict.** With training-amount controlled, `cka_drift` is larger in the forgetting arm (correct direction)
    but only ~1.3–2× and movement-contaminated; `cosine_drift` is movement-dominated (larger in replay regardless).
    Neither gate-worthy — but now mechanistically explained, not a "coin-flip." Recon corroborates via relative
    retention.
