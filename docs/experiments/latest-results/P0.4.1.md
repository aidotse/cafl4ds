# P0.4.1

From the [data + divergence substudy](phase0/P0.4.1.md). The data-driven companion to P0.4.0, the instability/divergence
mode (and Phase 0's instrument calibration) is now fully calibrated.

**Headline.** The gradient norm reads reconstruction-error magnitude (a *content* shock) — not distributional novelty. A
saturating out-of-range shock fires cleanly; an in-range distribution shift does not.

Two-sided change-point gate **PASS**. After warming the MAE to competence, a saturating-noise burst (`burst_scale=4`)
fires: burst loss **≈30×** and a grad-norm change point 78× (seed 0) / 74× (seed 1) the control's *same steps*, while
the base-only control stays flat. The spike then recovers to the base band in the tail — a large severity with zero
permanence.

- **Distributional OOD does not fire.** An in-range distributional burst gives reconstruction error comparable to the
    base and no separable grad-norm change point : color CIFAR, grayscale, phase-scrambled (structure destroyed, yet
    loss *drops* — smooth texture is easier to inpaint), in-range noise — all at/below the natural grad-norm variance
    floor (~2–4×). MAE recon difficulty tracks local inpaintability, not novelty.

- **Severity dose-response (fixed `lr_op=1`, on the shock amplitude; full ladder seeded 0/1/2).** `scale 1` no fire →
    `2/4` fire and recover (transient, seed-robust) → threshold near `scale 6` → runaway. Past the threshold the
    lead-time↔severity coupling is now **seed-stable across ~7 runaway rungs × 3 seeds** (median lead `8`→11 … `32`→4,
    monotone), no longer the original n=2 — and it is *bounded*: the lead **floors at ~4 steps** (earliest NaN is 4
    after burst onset even at a `32×` shock), while `scale 6` is a seed-fragile knife-edge (1/3 seeds runs away, lead
    70). So the operating envelope has a shape: lead longest at the threshold, floored deep past it. NaN in the base
    tail shows the shock *triggered* the runaway.

- **Negative control — LR-divergence is shock-independent.** In the `lr_sweep` ladder, shock and control go non-finite
    at the identical step (8 at `lr=3`, 5 at `lr=10`) — warm-phase LR divergence (P0.4.0), long before the step-300
    burst. Only a large out-of-range *content* shock triggers divergence at a base-stable LR.

- **Two nested vehicle findings.** P0.4.0: the loss must be **unbounded** (MAE MSE, not SimSiam cosine). P0.4.1:
    *within* MAE it must be **raw** (`norm_pix_loss=false`) — per-patch normalization caps the loss near 1.0, erasing
    the magnitude surprise needs. The `norm_pix`-on counterfactual is now preserved (`norm_pix_seed0`): the same
    saturating shock that fires `78×` under raw MSE gives only a **1.47×** blip, so the gate correctly does not fire.

- **The Phase-1 design recommendation (shared with P0.4.0).** Both grad-norm reads — the leading indicator (P0.4.0) and
    this surprise change-point — exist only under raw MSE + an undamped optimizer, and both are preserved at both poles
    (`adamw_seed0` / `norm_pix_seed0`). To keep the early-warning value, favour undamped + raw MSE; under the standard
    AdamW + `norm_pix` MAE, grad norm is a bare coincident NaN alarm and early-warning is a Phase-1 open problem.
