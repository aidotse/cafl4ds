## P0.3.4

From the [supervised forgetting upper-limit substudy](phase0/P0.3.4.md). Closes out the (failed) campaign to induce
forgetting on the SSL/MAE backbone and sanity-checks that the accuracy-matrix instruments (BWT/FM) react correctly in
the **canonical, supervised** catastrophic-forgetting setup. An **upper limit / necessary check — not** the required
calibration (instrument + MAE, label-free), which moves to [P0.3.6](phase0/P0.3.6.md).

**Result: every attempt to make MAE forget failed (the clean architectural finding); a guaranteed fire is obtained only
in the supervised native regime, via a *pretrained* deep-well backbone.**

- **MAE (SSL) — 0/5 fire, forgetting-resistant even on CIFAR-100.** Task A well learned *and* task B genuinely learns,
    yet B-only training leaves task A flat/slightly-up. SSL/MAE resists catastrophic forgetting on the canonical
    benchmark — after the toy-STL-10 exhaustion ([P0.3.0](phase0/P0.3.0.md)–[P0.3.3](phase0/P0.3.3.md)). (Scoped to
    these *short, near-IID, task-swap* conditions; correlation × long horizon is the open Phase-1 question —
    [P0.3.8](phase0/P0.3.8.md) probes one cheap lever.)
- **Supervised from-scratch — borderline both ways.** Overfits 4k images → R00 ≈ 0.58 (shallow well); best case PC BWT
    but healthy crosses the quiet bar / contrast < 2×.
- **Supervised, pretrained ViT-B/16 — the clean fire.** `skip_phase_a=true` keeps a pretrained backbone as phase A →
    (~9× deeper R00 well). N=5 @ `lr=1e-4`: **PC BWT ≤ −0.05 in 4/5**, **healthy holds 5/5**, contrast 3–31×, monotonic
    LR dose-response. The transferable lever (for conditions at this scale): crater depth is bounded by R00, so a deep
    *pretrained* phase-A well is the precondition for a fire.
- **Drift on the (supervised) fire.** `cka_drift` separates cleanly/correctly 5/5; `cosine_drift` stays inverted.
    Reported, not gated.

**What it did NOT close (by construction):** (1) whether an *MAE* backbone can be driven to a genuine crater at all
(here it never forgot); (2) whether the **label-free** monitors (`cka_drift`, MAE recon loss) — the only signals in
deployment — track a *label-free SSL* fire (the CKA↔forgetting relation here is a *supervised* one). Both are the target
of **P0.3.6** (SSL-MAE fire), reusing the pretrained-deep-well lever with a pretrained MAE backbone.

Promoted the N=5 comparison.jsons + seed-0 dose-response to [`artifacts/P0.3.4/`](phase0/artifacts/P0.3.4/).
