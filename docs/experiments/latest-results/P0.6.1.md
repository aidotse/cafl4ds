## P0.6.1

From [the *past-data-free* drift reader on the joint-embedding vehicle](phase0/P0.6.1.md). The companion to the
[P0.6.0](phase0/P0.6.0.md) anchor: the P0.6 pair splits the MAE forgetting gap into two orthogonal axes, and where
P0.6.0 targets **A1** (a *trustworthy* label-free reader, read on a retained task-A canary), **P0.6.1 targets C3** — a
reader that keeps **no task-A data at all**, so it can run on a genuinely transient stream.

**Status: complete — a *qualified* C3 success; the past-data-free reader survives, but only as projector *frame-churn*,
not the backbone content-drift that carried A1.**

The reader is **representation drift on a current-stream reference**: snapshot the model's embeddings of a phase-B batch
once, at the moment the task-A well is competent (post-A), then measure how far the live embeddings of that same batch
have moved — at both surfaces (backbone and the projector). It stores a fixed vector set, needs **no retained task-A
data**, and evaluates on **current-stream** inputs — a deployable rolling monitor. Same JE vehicle and config as P0.6.0
(re-run with the current-stream reader attached), with the frozen-probe BWT as ground truth and `rankme_proj` as the
collapse-specificity precondition (which holds throughout — the crater is forgetting, not collapse).

Two findings. **(1) A past-data-free reader exists, delivering C3.** Coordinate-frame drift at the projector separates
the forgetting arm from the matched non-forgetting arm two-sided **on every seed** — the first forgetting reader that
keeps no task-A data at all. **(2) Dropping the canary costs a surface and a metric.** The backbone content-drift that
was the *seed-stable A1 winner* on the task-A canary does **not** port past-data-free — it separates only on some seeds
and even *inverts* on one. The cause is the reference swap: pinned to a far-domain phase-B batch, the healthy arm's
backbone churns on that unfamiliar input too, so backbone content-drift converges across the arms and loses the
forgetting signal. What survives is the projector — SimSiam's most plastic surface — read through *frame-churn* rather
than content-drift, because that reads the PC arm's actual respecialization onto B rather than the shared far-domain
response.

The honest limitation, named up front and unchanged: drift reads representation *change*, not forgetting — on this
vehicle part of the PC arm's current-stream drift is benign "learning B" plasticity, and the two-sided control is what
holds that fixed so the residual is the forgetting-linked part. So this is *calibration*, not a standalone monitor: in
deployment there is no control to subtract, and disambiguating a live drift spike (degradation vs. benign reorganization
— the P0.1.0 handover question) is a Phase-1 problem.
