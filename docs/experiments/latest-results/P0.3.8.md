## P0.3.8

From the [hard-disjoint-distribution substudy](phase0/P0.3.8.md). Two in-regime probes at the P0.3.6/P0.3.7 fire
operating point, varying **only the phase-B distribution**: **(S)** phase-scrambled CIFAR (structure destroyed) to
attempt the genuine (savings ≈ 0) pole, **(G)** grayscale CIFAR (cosmetic) to test the MAE recon-loss reader for a false
positive. Both arms read the full gate + recon gap + `cka` + the P0.3.7 savings probe.

**Result: the hard-disjoint lever did not deepen the crater (MAE resistance strengthens on a genuinely harder shift),
and the recon-loss reader false-fires ~10× on a cosmetic shift.**

- **Arm S — the crater did not deepen.** The gate fires two-sided and the recon gap even widens, **but the crater depth
    is unchanged** despite destroying the spatial structure task A depends on, and phase-scramble is a *harder* task B
    that learns less. So the shallow crater is not for lack of a demanding phase B: the hard shift simply did not
    produce a genuine (deep) forgetting event. (The savings fraction again reads 1.0, but — as in
    [P0.3.7](phase0/P0.3.7.md) — that is degenerate on a crater this shallow and carries no weight; the verdict rests on
    crater depth.) The genuine pole is therefore neither populated nor testable here → **Phase-1** (correlation ×
    horizon).
- **Arm G — recon-loss FALSE POSITIVE confirmed.** The recon gap balloons to **+0.301** (~10× the color baseline's
    +0.036) while the probe BWT (−0.065) sits exactly at the cosmetic baseline. Mechanism: the PC decoder adapts its
    *output* to grayscale, so held-out reconstruction of *color* Imagenette craters (PC recon_A 0.541→0.842) while the
    replay arm's stays flat — a **decoder-output-distribution shift, not representation forgetting**. The two-sided
    replay control is what exposes it.

**Bounds on the label-free reader:** the recon-loss gap is a faithful forgetting signal only when phase B is *not*
itself an output-distribution shift versus the task-A it is scored on; a large recon gap with a quiet probe/savings is
the tell. `cka_drift` still does not transfer (tiny/near-noise even under a hard shift). This does not change the mode
verdict (✅ rests on the two-sided gate); it *characterizes the limits* — resistance is robust in-regime, and the trusted
recon reader has a bounded trust region.
