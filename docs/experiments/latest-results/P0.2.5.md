## P0.2.5

From the [partial-collapse dose-response sub-study](phase0/P0.2.5.md). Every earlier collapse sub-study drives SimSiam
to a *forced* trivial optimum — the thermometer has only ever been dipped in ice and boiling water, never checked for a
scale in between. P0.2.5 builds a controlled partial-collapse continuum at the certified corner and asks whether the
projector detectors read *incipient* (mild, gradual) severity — the early-warning case Phase-1 actually needs. It's a
**falsification / go-no-go probe**, not a ✅-gate, and it closes three audit gaps off one experiment: C1 (incipient
monotonicity), C2 (does the projector surface track the backbone we care about), C4 (an independent downstream anchor
for "healthy"). Two mechanistically distinct knobs (a loss blend and a soft stop-gradient) reach collapse by different
paths, so agreement between them is a genuine robustness test.

1. **The response is a smooth, monotone ramp — once the severity axis is resolved finely enough.** A coarse 5-rung
    ladder *looked* like a sharp step (the whole healthy→collapsed drop fell inside its first, unsampled interval), but
    a refined low-severity sweep across that interval recovers a continuous descent through genuinely intermediate
    states. The apparent cliff was an artifact of too-coarse (too-aggressive) severity spacing; both mechanistically
    distinct knobs and both seeds agree on the ramp — the pre-registered "both monotone" outcome.

1. **C1 — the projector detectors read the incipient band monotonically (early warning is demonstrable at toy scale).**
    On the resolved ladder RankMe@proj is monotone in severity in every run (and the other projector geometry
    instruments agree), so the detectors track a graded healthy→mild→collapsed scale, not just the forced extreme. The
    C1 gap is **closed** for the projector surface, strengthening the "read at the projector" choice. The narrower
    Phase-1 question is whether *real-scale* collapse presents as gradual a ramp as this toy vehicle does.

1. **C2 — the projector surface does track the backbone.** Across the (steppy) degradation, RankMe@proj correlates
    positively with both backbone geometry and backbone probe accuracy, so "read at the projector" does not betray the
    backbone we actually care about — a reassuring answer to the object-of-study mismatch, caveated by the step shape.

1. **C4 — downstream accuracy falls healthy → collapsed.** The backbone probe accuracy drops from the healthy to the
    collapsed pole, an independent anchor that "healthy" is genuinely useful (not just RankMe vouching for itself); the
    drop is modest, and the backbone stays above chance even at full projector collapse (it is *less* collapsed than
    the projector, consistent with the surface story).

The upshot: P0.2.5 **strengthens** the collapse instrument choice. A low-severity refinement (prompted by asking whether
the coarse "cliff" was just too-aggressive spacing) resolves the transition into a smooth monotone ramp, the projector
detectors read the incipient band, they track the backbone (C2), and utility confirms the axis (C4). The collapse ✅ now
covers graded severity, not only the forced extreme — with the Phase-1 handover narrowed to whether real-scale collapse
degrades as gradually as this toy vehicle does.
