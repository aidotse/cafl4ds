## P0.2.3

From the [redundancy-collapse sub-study](phase0/P0.2.3.md) — closing the *fire* side of off-diagonal covariance, the one
half of the collapse suite P0.2.2 could not reach.

**Status: complete — the fire side of `offdiag_cov` is closed.** A Barlow Twins vehicle (`cafl4ds/ssl/barlow.py`) with
its redundancy-reduction term ablated by a one-toggle `anti_collapse` switch, gated by a new `gate=redundancy_collapse`
criterion, robustly fires `offdiag_cov` under redundancy/dimensional collapse while the per-dimension-variance
discriminator stays quiet — the two-sided verdict P0.2.2 left open.

- **Result (4 seeds, 40-epoch IID STL-10, projector surface, min-across-seeds).** `offdiag_cov` **fires** ≥ 13.4× (PC
    ~0.007 vs healthy ~0.0004; bar 2.0) and `mean_feature_var` **stays quiet** at ≤ 1.12× (bar 2.0) — the direct
    discriminator confirming this is redundancy, not point, collapse. RankMe corroborates (≥ 17×) and uniformity fires
    (gap ≥ 2.40). All four seeds PASS the `redundancy_collapse` gate.
- **The fingerprint is surface-specific.** At the **projector** surface (terminal `BatchNorm(affine=False)` pins
    per-feature variance) the signature is the textbook "off-diagonal ↑, variance flat." At the **backbone** surface (no
    BN pin) `offdiag_cov` stays *quiet* (≤ 0.22×) while variance instead drops (2.8–4.6×) — so `offdiag_cov`'s fire is a
    projector-surface phenomenon, vindicating the both-surfaces readout.
- **Second independent collapse sub-mode.** The point-collapse instruments respond *differently* here than under SimSiam
    (variance is preserved at the projector, not craters everywhere), substantiating P0.2.2's "no strict proxy /
    complementary-coverage" claim with a fresh data point: `offdiag_cov` is now calibrated on **both** sides.
