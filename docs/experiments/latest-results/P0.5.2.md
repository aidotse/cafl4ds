## P0.5.2

From [the full-rank degradation crater & the candidate MAE-native readers](phase0/P0.5.2.md), the single successor to
[P0.5.0](phase0/P0.5.0.md)/[P0.5.1](phase0/P0.5.1.md) and the constructive close of the P0.5 mode. Those two substudies
confirmed the P0.2 suite's **quality blind spot on an MAE backbone** but could not *induce* the mode at toy scale — and
a candidate quality reader cannot be calibrated without a genuine difference in quality to track. This substudy supplies
that degradation and puts the candidate MAE-native readers on trial against it.

**Reframed success criterion (the load-bearing decision).** Success is a real, *full-rank* degradation, not a
near-chance crater. This mode is defined by the *dissociation* — the cheap online signals (loss, RankMe) read healthy
while the frozen probe falls — so keeping RankMe high matters more than crater depth: a deeper crater bought by
collapsing the rank is not this mode induced harder, it is a leak into P0.2 collapse. The severity bar comes down; the
reliability bar (seed-robust, min-across-N) and reader specificity bar (fire two-sided, quiet on a healthy-vs-healthy
null) go up in its place.

**Status: runs complete (5-seed verdict landed); mode-status label is the reviewer's call.** The wells pretrain to
genuine competence (clearing the P0.5.0/P0.5.1 headroom wall). **Degrade-from-competent *resists*** (a pretrained
competent MAE is sticky — the P0.3 lesson again), so the degradation is induced **from-scratch at the now-competent
scale** (the P0.5.0 design, revived by scale). The vehicle is the over-powered-decoder lever: it degrades the probe
while holding rank, where the mask lever is too gentle and the combined lever deepens the crater only by *collapsing*
the rank (P0.2 territory). The **capacity gate is refuted** — shrinking the encoder buys a deeper crater only by eroding
RankMe, not at full rank — so MAE's full-rank corner is **shallow but real**.

**The two robust deliverables are the negatives; the positive reader is a candidate.** Across 5 seeds the dissociation
*direction* is seed-robust — RankMe preserved and the loss actually *lower* on the degraded arm every seed, while the
frozen probe is worse every seed — though the crater *magnitude* is shallow (min +0.028 / mean +0.052, 2/5 seeds within
noise, and the induction gate `passed` on only 2/5, so the gate is a means, not the verdict). The robust, portable
result is a pair of **negatives**: on an MAE backbone the whole P0.2 geometry suite is a *trap* — RankMe blind by
construction, and `uniformity`/`clusterability` **actively mis-signal** (they call the degraded rep *healthier*, large
and consistent every seed on both levers), a first-class deployable "do-not-trust-geometry-readers-on-MAE" warning; and
a strong MAE **resists** the shortcut, so the full-rank degradation is only ever *shallow*. On the positive side,
**`alignment` under a stronger augment fires two-sided on all 5 seeds** (~2.7× a seed-noise null) where that suite is
blind — but at *one* operating point only: the crater-hunt grid shows it keys on the over-powered-decoder **lever**, not
the probe crater (quiet on full-rank mask/continue craters of equal-or-greater depth), so it is a **promising
candidate** calibrated as a binary regime detector, not yet an established quality reader. Honest caveats: the
degradation is **shallow / magnitude-variable**, the reader reads the shortcut **regime** not the quality *magnitude*
and is **single-vehicle** (quality-vs-lever unresolved), and the deployable panel reduces to **one** reader — so
magnitude-calibration, permanence, scale, and the defining **cross-vehicle test** are Phase-1. Built-in trap: RankMe
high is the *precondition* (full-rank), not a fire. Scoped as a single substudy absorbing the former P0.5.2–P0.5.5
idea-slots.
