## P0.2.2

From the [full collapse-metric calibration sub-study](phase0/P0.2.2.md) — extending P0.2.1's RankMe gate to the rest of
the joint-embedding collapse suite (per-dim variance, off-diag covariance, uniformity, alignment), read at both of
SimSiam's surfaces (backbone encoder and projector), to map where each instrument fires and where it stays quiet.

1. **The rest of the collapse suite is now calibrated, and the instruments complement each other — none is a strict
    proxy for another, but the complementarity is in *coverage*, not magnitude.** Across the regime map (E1) each
    standalone instrument×surface pair shows a *distinct* robust firing pattern. A post-hoc independence check (E7,
    Spearman across all 88 E1–E4 runs) confirms no pair is a strict proxy (max ρ ≈ 0.93) yet the projector trio is
    *strongly* rank-correlated — so multivariate monitoring is justified because the instruments cover different
    **firing regimes**, not because their magnitudes are independent.

1. **The measurement surface is the single biggest lever on sensitivity — collapse is legible at the projector,
    attenuated at the backbone.** RankMe @ proj fires in *every* regime corner (single-pass, correlated, long-horizon),
    rescuing the single-pass corner P0.2.1's backbone gate could not certify. Backbone-surface variance/uniformity are
    null everywhere; the projector is where the collapse fingerprint lives. Phase-1 geometry monitoring should read at
    the projector, keeping the backbone reading only as the downstream-relevant reference.

1. **The certified P0.2.1 gate (RankMe @ backbone) is doubly constrained — it needs an IID stream *and* a long
    horizon.** A correlation sweep (E3) shows it drops from 4/4 to 0/4 the instant any same-class blocking appears (the
    healthy arm goes low-rank too), and a horizon sweep (E2) shows it only fires robustly from ≈ 25 epochs. A
    single-pass LR sweep (E4) pins shows 0/4 at every LR (0.25×–4×), so a hotter optimizer does not substitute for the
    missing epochs. The projector-surface pair (RankMe, uniformity) is both the correlation-invariant and the early
    core — it fires from ~5 epochs and at every correlation level. For Phase-1's short-horizon, non-IID streams, a lone
    backbone gate would be too slow and too fragile.

1. **`mean_feature_var @ proj` is the sharpest-on-a-typical-seed but least-reliable detector.** It reaches ~30× on a
    good draw but its worst-of-seeds margin sits right on the 2× bar (and dips *below* it at one correlation setting),
    so it is a real but noisy instrument — never a standalone gate at this toy scale. E8 (seed-replicate
    estimator-variance) puts a number on it: it is the noisiest instrument (CoV 0.8–1.7) and the *only* reported FIRE
    that is noise-limited; every other reported FIRE, including the certified gate in its regime, is statistically safe
    (P = 0).

1. **Off-diag covariance is a confirmed null everywhere** (both surfaces, every horizon and correlation level) — the
    *correct* outcome, since point-collapse does not produce the redundancy/dimensional collapse the instrument
    targets. This keeps the metric↔failure-mode mapping honest and defines a concrete follow-up — a redundancy-collapse
    vehicle (Barlow Twins / VICReg), now its own sub-study [P0.2.3](phase0/P0.2.3.md) — not a gap in this study.

This calibrates the suite against **point** collapse (P0.2 → P0.2.1 → P0.2.2), with the post-hoc independence (E7) and
estimator-variance (E8) checks now done. Two named follow-ups remain before the collapse suite is fully certified: the
*fire* side of off-diag covariance (so far calibrated quiet-side only) needs a redundancy-collapse vehicle
(Barlow/VICReg), now promoted to its own sub-study **[P0.2.3](phase0/P0.2.3.md)** since it is a new SSL-method add; and
**E6** — false-positive stress on the quiet side (healthy-but-atypical representations), spec'd in the
[P0.2.2 sub-study doc](phase0/P0.2.2.md). The remaining Phase-0 gates then cover the *forgetting* (P0.3) and
*instability* (P0.4) failure modes.
